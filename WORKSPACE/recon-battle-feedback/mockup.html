When a shot achieves nothing, can the player tell why?

Four presentations of the same event: an RPG round striking an Abrams frontally. Nominal warhead damage 6000; delivered damage 4285, because Penetration: 500 is under the frontal armour value of 700 (weapons-ballistics.yaml:535, vehicles-america.yaml:497-500). The motivating case in the brief is the same mechanism, one rung worse: raw damage 54000 with Penetration left unset, so it defaults to 1 (DamageWarhead.cs:24) and a T-90's 280 mm divides it down to 192 — 0.8% of that tank's 24000 HP. Pinned in BallisticPenetrationTest.UnsetPenetrationGutsDamageAgainstArmour.

Every colour is from mods/ww3mod/chrome/_lobby-palette.yaml. Presentation choices are marked [TASTE] in the written proposal; nothing here is a shipped design.

Why INotifyDamage cannot answer “why”

The obvious hook is the wrong one, and this is the single fact that sets the cost of every option above. AttackInfo (engine/OpenRA.Game/Traits/TraitsInterfaces.cs:80-86) carries only the final number:

In AttackInfo Damage.Value (final), Attacker, DamageState, PreviousDamageState. That is the whole payload. A listener sees -192 and cannot tell a small weapon from a defeated one.
Only at the warhead DamageWarhead.InflictDamage (:220-257) has Damage (raw), damage (post-penetration), thickness, armorPercent, Penetration, DamageVersus(...) and modifiedDamage all live in one scope. The ratio raw → final is the signal, and it exists at exactly one site.
Already formatted GunTrace writes precisely this breakdown — rawDamage, afterThickness, thickness, pen, versus, FINAL, hpBefore — at DamageWarhead.cs:248-249. It goes to debug.log behind WW3_GUNTRACE=1. The explanation is computed in the shipped build today and thrown away.

Two shipped precedents that constrain the design

The heal flash WithHealFlash ships and works — measured at (50,98,183) → (67,137,255), a clean 1.4× lift. And it was still at the threshold of perceptibility: on a ~15 px sprite for 360 ms it could not be told from the control by eye, only under 12× magnification (WORKSPACE/medic-heal-flash-vs-control.png). Any tint-based cue on infantry inherits that risk. A tank is a bigger sprite, which is the case that matters here.
The critical pip Blink rate is already a readout of remaining health, ramped so every part of its range reads (2054b7b0). It is the house precedent for analog, non-numeric, carried on artwork — which is the argument for option B over option A.

The separate product question: the health bar

None of the four options restores accumulated damage legibility, because the mod has no health bar by choice. A 0.8% hit lights one pip and then 31 further hits change nothing on screen. Turning the bar back on is the largest single legibility gain available — and the engine file asks, in as many words, that it not be done without asking: “Re-enabling this is a product decision, not a cleanup: it turns on an indicator the user switched off himself. Ask before uncommenting.” (SelectionBarsAnnotationRenderable.cs:180-181). It is raised here as a question, not proposed as work.
