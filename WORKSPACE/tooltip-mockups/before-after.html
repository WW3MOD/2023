Each weapon is a section, and each price is authored once. The rocket's 50 now appears exactly once, inside the section for the weapon that spends it. The prose keeps only its sentence; every figure is emitted by the trait that owns it, so the two can no longer disagree.

The rocket is 91% of this man's refill bill — 50 of 55. On the left that ratio is unrecoverable without doing the arithmetic yourself; here the two sections state it.

55 supply is now a row, not a remainder. It has its own label and the meter answers the question the player is actually asking — how much of my depot is this? Forty-one rifleman refills empty a full Centre.

Nothing here is new data. Speed 25 and Health 200 are Mobile.Speed / Health.HP; 2250 is LOGISTICSCENTER.SupplyProvider.TotalSupply (structures.yaml:466). Only the role subhead (NATO · LINE INFANTRY) would need authoring — flagged as a taste call in the audit.

Armour is the one row that must NOT be auto-bound. This rifleman's Armor.Type is literally Kevlar (infantry.yaml:175, inherited by all 28 infantry) — but Kevlar appears in zero warhead Versus: tables, so every weapon applies its default 100%. The prose's "No armor" is therefore mechanically correct and the trait string is the phantom. Printing Armour: Kevlar from the trait would replace a true statement with one implying protection that does not exist.
