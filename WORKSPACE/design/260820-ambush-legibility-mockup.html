Ambush legibility — proposed readout

2026-08-20 · research mockup, nothing built · companion to 260820-ambush-legibility.md

The question this mockup exists to settle: the user asked for a white “!” meaning “this soldier is blocked from acting normally, because they are hiding.” Below: what that would look like, what it should mean, and the two ways it could sit next to the red “!” that already ships.

Drawn at roughly 2× game scale so the glyphs are readable. Slot offsets are the real ones from defaults.yaml:795-807, doubled. In game, Position: Top negates margin.X — so a negative X in YAML puts the mark to the right of centre.

3 · Where the white “!” goes — the decision to make

Both options are cheap. They differ in what happens when a soldier is both hiding and seen.

OPTION 1 — SHARE THE SLOT (recommended)
!
!
One mark at −8, 0. Colour carries the meaning: white = hiding and it is working; red = you are seen. Red takes precedence.
Reads as one axis: “the ! lane means your visibility needs attention; the colour says which way.” Costs no new screen real estate.
OPTION 2 — TAKE THE FREE SLOT
!
!
White at −16, 0, red stays at −8, 0. Both can show at once: “I halted to hide — and it failed, they see me anyway.”
Strictly more information, at the cost of two same-shaped glyphs side by side, which prior capture work found reads as clutter on a 34px box.
OPTION 3 — GENERALISE (not yet)
!
White ! = not executing your order, whatever the cause — hiding, pinned, no path, out of ammo — with the reason named on hover.
Presented, not recommended. But the halt state should be built with a reason field so Option 1 can grow into this without a rewrite.
Dependency. The white “!” needs a real “stopped because hiding” state, and it does not exist for human players. The nearest thing — haltedForAmbush in AttackMoveActivity — is gated behind enable-ambush-tactics, granted only to units the AI posts. It must also be readable from the render path and must not be a granted condition: driving visibility marks from conditions is the shape of two shipped desyncs in this repo.

4 · The per-observer problem — why “hidden” is not obvious

A soldier can be hidden from one enemy and visible to another. So what should one icon mean — hidden from all, from the nearest, or from any?

!
scout — sees you
tank — does not
Two observers, two different answers. One glyph cannot say both.

The answer: do not invent a rule — inherit the shipped one

The red “!” already solves this, carefully. Its rule is: enemies within 32c0 · only those you are already aware of · reduction = any · truth-gated last.

So hidden must be the exact complement of spotted — same observer set, same knowledge filter, same any reduction, negated. If the two marks used different observer sets they could be lit together or dark together, and the player would be right to trust neither.

One addition, or the mark is vacuous. !spotted is also true for a soldier alone in the backfield with no enemy for thirty cells. That is not hiding, it is being elsewhere. So the mark must mean: at least one enemy I am aware of is in range, AND none of them can see me. One extra counter in a loop that already runs.

Per-observer detail (“hidden from the tank, seen by the scout”) is a two-body fact and a badge is a one-body surface. If it is ever wanted it belongs to the gauge or a panel — never to a glyph.

5 · Defect — at maximum concealment the gauge draws nothing

The gauge draws a grey ring at the range from which a standard observer first sees you. Stopping, going prone and digging in shrink it. But at the top of the ladder it vanishes.

MOVING · visibility 2
Sniper on the move. Seen from 25 cells.
PRONE + DUG IN · visibility 7
Stopped for 12 s. Ring shrinks to 10 cells — the feedback loop works, and it is good.
+ COVER · visibility 10 — TODAY
— nothing drawn —
The reward for doing everything right is a blank screen. Undetectable and “feature broken” render identically.
PROPOSED FIX
A distinct innermost marker — dashed and faintly filled — meaning “no standard vision can see you at any range.” Costs one tier.
This is reachable, and by exactly the ambush units. Sniper and Special Forces have base Vision: 5; 5 + 3 (cover) + 1 (prone) + 1 (dug in) = 10, the clamp ceiling. A standard rifleman (base 3) tops out at 8 and always draws a ring — so this only bites the units the player ambushes with, in the situation they set up for.

6 · Defect — the cover bonus only comes from burnt trees

LIVE FOREST — +0 concealment
🌲 🌲
Living trees grant no object-proximity. They carry Density, which reduces damage — a different system.
BURNT HUSKS — +3 concealment
🌲 🌲
Only ^TreeHusk carries ProximityExternalCondition@ObjectProximity — the sole granter repo-wide.
The largest term in the concealment stack is unobtainable in the terrain built for it. object-proximity (+1..+3) has exactly one granter in the whole repo: ^TreeHusk in husks.yaml:118, inherited by the 21 *.Husk tree actors. decoration.yaml, which defines the living trees T01–T17 and TC01–TC05, contains zero proximity traits. You must burn the forest down before it hides you.

Very likely inverted by accident, and a strong candidate for the root of “I am not sure what is actually active in game.” Recorded in WORKSPACE/bugs/discovered.md; not fixed on this branch, which changes no behaviour. Note the interaction: fixing this makes the §5 blank-screen case common rather than rare, so §5 should be fixed first or together.

7 · The part that actually answers the complaint — the ledger

The user did not ask “am I hidden?”. They said they cannot tell what is active. Five automatic modifiers decide concealment; the player commands none of them and the game reports none of them. Showing the ledger on the selected unit is the fix.
