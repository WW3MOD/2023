Lobby chrome — color options (all neutral / grayscale-leaning)

Six palettes applied to the same lobby fragment so they can be compared head-to-head. All keep the bevel mechanic, the panel geometry, and the V5 player rows — only the colors change. Faction colors are pulled toward muted versions across every palette so the chrome doesn't compete with team identity.

The CTA button stays green in every option (the only "go" signal on the page); ready ticks share that green. Everything else is the palette's neutral.

Slate panels with a Westwood-amber accent on tabs, links, and headers. Most "tactical-radio" feel. Amber and green coexist (amber = info, green = go).
