_**ONE OF THE TWO CANDIDATE CAUSES IS NOW DEAD — checked 2026-08-15 on `wt/combined-arms`. The `transportModuleResolved` latch is BENIGN and should stop being carried as a suspect.** The condition it depends on is granted in `INotifyCreated.Created` (`GrantConditionOnBotOwner.cs:44`) — i.e. at actor creation, **before any tick** — so `IsTraitDisabled` is already false by the time `TryFerryCapture` first resolves the module.
