Nuclear arsenal — what each option actually ends up as

Against main @ aaafec23. Shipped values are measured from the weapon files; anything tagged NEW is a proposal and exists nowhere yet.

First, the thing that changes the question: there are no factions here.

Not one power in powers.yaml carries a Prerequisites: line — zero across all ten. Every player can buy every power, whichever side they picked. The file states the opposite: the comment above the conventional tier says the Kinzhal and the GBU-57 are "faction-locked to opposite sides, so no player ever holds both", and the case for pricing them identically rests on that. The lock was never built. So the two columns below are a proposal, not a description — picking any option also means deciding whether to build the gating that makes it true.

The tactical rung is broken, and it decides one of the two answers

The price ladder claimed the tactical nuke had a ~30-cell blast in two places. It has 15 — it was resized from 30c to 15c when it was given its 20 kt yield, and the ladder was never updated. So the entry costing 15 000 is smaller than the one costing 13 000 (15.0c against the B61-12's 24.5c). It is not a rung; it is a hole. Both numbers are corrected in the tree now, but the pricing consequence is a balance decision, not a comment fix.

The tactical slot — three ways

T1 · Retire it ← recommended

Drop it from the Powers tab; Atomic stays as Dead Hand's warhead and the autotest scenario's. The band is already covered at 0.3, 50 and 100 kt.

  • Removes the non-monotone rung rather than repricing it
  • Ten powers becomes nine, against a palette that binds only six hotkeys
  • Costs the 20 kt step, which nothing else occupies

T2 · Replace it with the 10 kt B61 that already exists

NukeB61Mod12Y10 is built, shipped and fired by nothing. Wiring it up as B61-12 (10 kt) needs no new weapon at all.

  • A real weapon, a real yield, and a genuine rung between 0.3 and 50 kt
  • Cheapest option in the file — a power block pointed at an existing weapon
  • Gives the US a fifth nuclear entry against Russia's two, widening the imbalance

T3 · Split it per faction

Two entries at that rung: B61-12 (10 kt) for NATO and 9M729 Iskander-M (10–50 kt) for BRICS.

  • Only option that keeps the rung and balances the sides
  • Requires the faction gating to exist, or both are buyable by everyone
  • A single both-faction name at 20 kt has no honest candidate — the W80-4 is US-only

Two decisions hiding underneath

New weapons, or re-yield the old ones in place?

  • New weapons — the powers point at fresh definitions and Atomic / AtomicHighYield stay untouched, still fired by Dead Hand and the autotest. Nothing outside the Powers tab moves.
  • Re-yield in place — fewer files, but Atomic is what Dead Hand throws at all twelve derricks and test-heavy-strike-erases-economy fires it too. Dead Hand was retuned hours ago.

I would take new weapons. That coupling is invisible where you edit and expensive where you find it.

Yield and blast radius are authored separately

Changing a stated yield does not move the blast — MaxRadius is written per weapon. Any yield move above is therefore a deliberate balance edit with a radius to re-derive, not a label change that carries its numbers along.

Where I would land

Retire the tactical, name the strategic DF-5B, build the faction gating. Retiring is the only option that fixes the broken rung instead of repricing it, and the 10 kt B61 stays available if you later want that step back. DF-5B moves the yield least of any real weapon and puts a third BRICS nation on the board rather than making Russia carry everything. The gating is worth doing whichever way you go — without it, "the list for both factions" describes something the game does not currently do.
