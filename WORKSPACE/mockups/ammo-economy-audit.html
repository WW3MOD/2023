Branch wt/tooltip-economy, based on main @ 03c77208. Two commits: 22b2e9d4 naming, c4cb3c23 economics. Numbers are arithmetic over the shipped ammo pools (WORKSPACE/ammo_audit.py) — not simulation. dump-stats.sh cannot back them: the solution builds only OpenRA.Game and Mods.Common, and tools/combat-sim/data/stats.json carries no ammo-pool data.

The mechanic these numbers rest on

SupplyValue is charged per batch, not per round. AmmoPool.TryServeBatch deducts one flat SupplyValue and serves Min(ReloadCount, missing) rounds — so the per-round price is SupplyValue / ReloadCount, and a pool one round short still pays a whole batch. Verified by reading the method, not taken on trust.

1  The Iskander — what it actually does

The user hedged on the spawns-as-an-actor part. It is correct, and it matters for the name. IskanderTargeter is an InstantHit with Damage: 50 and every Versus at 0 — it hits nothing at all. The launcher's MissileSpawnerMaster (vehicles-russia.yaml:1082) releases the real IskanderMissile actor.

So the row counts two missiles, not two targeting cycles — confirmed independently by the two loaded-missile sprites, which gate on ammo-primary == 2 and ammo-primary. Renaming it to a missile is right, and "Ballistic missile" is what shipped.

2  Ammunition repricing — what changed

The BTR was real: 1 supply per 50 rounds = 0.02/round, against the automatic rifleman's 0.05/round (AR, 1 per 20). A heavy machine gun cartridge cost two fifths of a rifle round. It was not isolated — thirteen pools priced a larger round at or below the rifle anchor, including a 30mm autocannon shell at exactly a rifle cartridge's price.

The scale, and what is honest about it

Anchor held at 0.05/round for 5.56mm; everything larger placed above it by calibre:

7.62mm 0.08  |  12.7mm 0.16  |  20mm 0.23  |  25mm 0.28  |  30mm 0.32  |  GAU-8 DU 0.40

Compressed, not literal. Real cartridge costs run roughly 140× from 5.56mm to 30mm; applied literally to a 900-round magazine that makes an IFV cost more to rearm than to call in. What is preserved is the ordering and the rough ratios between classes — the user's 3× floor for the BTR is cleared at 3.2×.

The one place I had to overrule my own justification

The BTR was going to 0.20/round on the strength of its comment reading 14.5mm KPVT — which is correct for a real BTR-80. But its Armament fires 12.7mm.MG, the same weapon key the m113 fires. Pricing one round two ways by faction is the exact fault this pass removes, so the shipped data beat the comment and both sit at 8. The stale comment is corrected in place. If the 14.5mm distinction is wanted it needs its own weapon, not a different ammunition price on a shared one.

Consistency check

Every weapon key now carries exactly one per-round price, except two left deliberately for your ruling (items 2 and 3 below). Three keys were split before this pass; the 25mm.Bradley split — the identical round costing 2.5× less on the strykershorad than on the bradley — is closed.
