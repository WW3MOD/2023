Warhead sprite candidates

A search of the art that already exists, for something that reads as a re-entry vehicle or a free-fall bomb rather than as a missile. Nothing is rewired — this is a look before a decision. Base main @ 79c41028.

Sprites were extracted with OpenRA.Utility --png against temperat.pal, which is the file behind both the mod’s player palette (what these actors render with) and its effect palette — see rules/palettes.yaml. --noshadow was used, so ground-shadow pixels come out transparent instead of rendering as palette green.

The headline is a dead end. There is no cone anywhere in the asset set, and no finned bomb either — the Red Alert bomb sprite is a 2×2 pixel blob. What does exist is a handful of unfinned bodies with full 32-facing coverage, and exactly one parachute.

The facings problem

Every warhead sequence declares Facings: 32 and every warhead actor carries WithFacingSpriteBody (rules/defaults.yaml, ^ShootableMissile). A candidate with fewer facings does not fail to load — it does something worse. DefaultSpriteSequence.GetSprite indexes GetFacingFrameOffset(facing) * length + frame % length (DefaultSpriteSequence.cs:753-757), so with Facings unset the facing term is zero and the sprite animates through its frames while ignoring which way it is travelling. That is fatal for cmissdown and atomicdn, which would fall sideways-on regardless of their trajectory — and harmless for parabomb, because a parachute canopy seen from above has no heading to get wrong.

There is an escape hatch nobody in this mod uses: InterpolatedFacings (DefaultSpriteSequence.cs:151) makes the renderer rotate a low-facing sprite to fill in the gaps, and the rotation is applied on the ordinary render path (Animation.cs:62). It must be a power of two, greater than Facings, at most 1024. It costs image quality — rotated pixel art resamples — which is exactly the trade the brief anticipated: a cone viewed nose-on is nearly rotationally symmetric, so an RV survives it far better than a finned bomb would. Zero uses of it exist in mods/ww3mod/ today, so nobody has seen what it looks like in this mod.
