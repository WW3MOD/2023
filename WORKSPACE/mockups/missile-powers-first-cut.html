How interception actually works: guns cannot do it — Bullet aims where the target was when it fired, with no lead, so every cannon in the mod misses a fast missile by 10–12 cells. Only homing missiles lead. So the dial is speed: a MANPAD flies 450 and cannot catch anything faster; the AA vehicles' missiles fly 600; the (disabled) SAM flies 800. Make the cruise tier slow enough for 600 to close and the split the user asked for enforces itself.

“= tanks” prices each strike against a main battle tank (T-90 2400 / Abrams 2500, mean 2450). For scale: rifleman 100, Bradley 1500, Tunguska 1700, Stryker-SHORAD 2500, Iskander & HIMARS 6000 for two shots, nuclear silo 50000.

What the warheads actually do, by arithmetic over the shipped YAML (the combat-sim cannot answer this — it needs a build that does not exist and its committed stats are stale): IskanderExplosion does ~62,800 to an Abrams on a direct hit, against 28,000 HP — 2.24× overkill, and that is the shipped conventional ballistic missile. Atomic does ~266,000: a 6.25-cell airburst that vaporises to ~8.4 cells, kills infantry to ~23 and sets structures alight to ~28. A Kinzhal should read as one Iskander missile, without having to drive the launcher there. Past roughly 80,000 a conventional missile stops reading as a missile and starts reading as a small nuke.

Speeds, at 16.667 tps (cells/s = Speed × 0.016276): fast tier at Speed 2000 = 32.6 c/s, 3.8× an F-16, crossing 50 cells in 1.5 s. Cruise tier at Speed 350 = 5.7 c/s, 8.8 s over the same run — slower than every aircraft in the game. Shipped anchors: Iskander missile 600, HIMARS rocket 500, F-16 525, A-10 390.

None of them can touch the Supply Route. It carries TargetTypes: NoAutoTarget and no weapon in the mod lists it, so every warhead is rejected — including the nuke. These are tools for killing armies and buildings, never a win button.
