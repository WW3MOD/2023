The glyph trap this dodges. ◆ U+25C6 BLACK DIAMOND and ◇ U+25C7 WHITE DIAMOND — the obvious pair — are absent from this font's cmap; its entire Geometric Shapes block is missing. They would have rendered as nothing, silently. U+25CA LOZENGE and U+2666 BLACK DIAMOND SUIT are the only hollow/solid diamond pair the font actually carries, and both were confirmed to have real outlines (2 contours and 1 contour respectively).
