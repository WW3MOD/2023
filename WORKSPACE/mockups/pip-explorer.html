What this model does and does not claim

The concealment tiers are read from the shipped file: prone +1, dug-in +1, moving −1, firing −2 (infantry.yaml:771-787). The hit radii 30 → 20 and the 44% area ratio are HitShape@Cover (:143-150). The veto is AutoTarget.cs:765-781, on the ungated !stage3 path every human unit takes. The mapping from tiers to an "exposure" number is invented for this page — the engine resolves visibility per vision band against CurrentVisibility, not on a 0–100 scale. Treat the shape of the response as the claim, not the numbers on it.

Three things worth driving deliberately

1 · What the veto costs with no second channel. Set the modifier to N, Ambush to Holding, proximity around 70. The soldier is aimed at a target and deliberately not firing, and nothing on screen says so. That is the shipped game today.

2 · The one real overlap. Keep Holding, turn third party on. The pip goes red — someone sees him — while the veto stays shut, because the veto tests his target's vision, not everyone's. Colour carries one fact and the modifier the other; switch between H, R and S to compare.

3 · The case no pip can fix. Set Ambush to Sprung with third party off and proximity around 50. He is firing, and the pip correctly reads not-spotted, because he is not spotted. ambushTriggered is terminal. The readout is honest; the inference "not red, so not shooting" is what fails, and no visual scheme repairs that.
