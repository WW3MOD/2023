WW3MOD
A total conversion into a modern World War 3 real-time
strategy game: NATO and America against BRICS and Russia.


DEVELOPMENT

    * FreadyFish
    * CmdrBambi

MAPS

Several maps are adapted from OpenRA's Red Alert mod and keep
their original authors. Each one credits its author in the
map browser listing.


GAME CONTENT

WW3MOD loads artwork and audio from the Command & Conquer:
Red Alert data files. Those files are supplied by the player
from the 2008 freeware release or from an original copy of
the game.

This distribution also includes art and audio assets derived
from Command & Conquer games. No ownership of that material
is claimed; it remains the property of its owners.

Command & Conquer and Red Alert are trademarks of their
respective owners, who are not affiliated with this project.
