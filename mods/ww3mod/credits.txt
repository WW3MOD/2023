WW3MOD
A total conversion into a modern World War 3 real-time
strategy game: NATO and America against Russia.

Several maps are adapted from OpenRA's Red Alert mod and keep
their original authors. Each one credits its author in the
map browser listing.


GAME CONTENT

WW3MOD loads artwork and audio from the Command & Conquer:
Red Alert data files. Those files are supplied by the player
from the 2008 freeware release or from an original copy of
the game.

This distribution also includes art and audio assets derived
from Command & Conquer games. No ownership of that material
is claimed; it remains the property of its owners.

Command & Conquer and Red Alert are trademarks of their
respective owners, who are not affiliated with this project.


MUSIC

No music was composed for WW3MOD. The soundtrack you hear is
Red Alert's own, played from the score files in your copy of
the game; none of it is distributed with this mod.

One music file is included in this distribution:

    * journey.aud - believed to be from the Red Alert games.
      Its exact source has not been confirmed, so it is
      credited here as unattributed rather than guessed at.
      It is a placeholder and is intended to be replaced.

If your installation has no Red Alert score files, that one
track is the only music the game has, and it will loop.


ART

Almost all of the artwork you see is Red Alert's, loaded from
your own copy of the game, or is derived from Command &
Conquer artwork. It is placeholder art and is intended to be
replaced.

That includes the unit sprites - a number of modern vehicles
are currently drawn with the closest-looking Red Alert unit -
along with the build icons, the weapon effects and the
terrain. Where a Russian unit and its American counterpart
share an icon, that is a placeholder standing in for art that
has not been drawn yet.

    * Unit, icon, effect and terrain art - derived from
      Command & Conquer: Red Alert and other games in the
      series. No individual artist is credited because no
      per-file record of where each piece came from was kept;
      we would rather say so than invent an attribution.
    * Menu and sidebar artwork - reworked for WW3MOD from
      OpenRA's Red Alert chrome.
    * Some interface elements are still OpenRA's or Red
      Alert's unchanged, including the icon this mod shows in
      the mod chooser.

Fonts: FreeSans and FreeSansBold are GNU FreeFont. The
ZoodRangmah display face ships with the OpenRA engine.


SOUND

No sound effects or voice lines were recorded for WW3MOD.

Weapon, engine, explosion and interface sounds are Red
Alert's, played from your own copy of the game or included
here in the same derived form as the artwork above.

Unit voice lines are Red Alert's, in English, for both
sides - an American and a Russian soldier answer with the
same game's recordings. New voice work has not been done.

    * Sound effects and voice lines - derived from Command &
      Conquer: Red Alert and other games in the series, and
      credited as unattributed for the same reason as the
      art.
    * A few of the included sound sets match no naming scheme
      we recognise and we do not know where they came from.
      They are listed as unattributed rather than credited to
      anyone, and none is used by a unit in the game.
