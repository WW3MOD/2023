# The indicator set collapses to one glyph — the layout plan's central premise is superseded one hour after merging

_Recorded 2026-09-12T15:17:12.410Z by d994e671_

**User ruling, 2026-09-12, on question `9-tj4kZAoGix_LCMgMIiY`.** All four rows rejected with no picks. Verbatim: *"Neither of these are good. Sorry. It seems you didnt understand. I want each unit to have the floating diamond shape only, and I want to design that diamond shape in such a way that it can hold: Health/Suppression and if the unit is detected or not. Color could be for Suppression/health/impediment (or whatever we call it), and the degree that it is filled is for the level of detection."*

**The design is now settled and it is not a layout.** One mark per unit — the floating diamond. Colour encodes impediment. Fill degree encodes detection. What remains open is the drawing of that one glyph, and the user asked for variations to choose from: mostly small ones around their proposal, a few experiments, one or two out of the box.

**What went wrong, because it was my error and not the worker's.** The brief I wrote asked for *"the final layout for everything drawn over a unit"* and offered candidate design languages — a ground arc, a column, corner badges, sprite tint — as its worked example of "one row per style". The worker executed that brief well: the rendering was faithful, the disqualification of corner badges was a genuine finding, and three measurements it took corrected existing documents. The deliverable was still wrong, because **the user's "one row per style" meant variations of the mark, and I read it as alternative arrangements of the marks.** The tell was available before dispatch and I missed it: every prior ruling in this strand was about what a *glyph* can carry — detectability most visible, the sprite may carry state, the diamond spans 3 of 10 levels — and none was about where marks sit.

**General form worth carrying:** when the user says "styles", establish what the unit of variation is before writing the brief. A brief that picks the wrong unit of variation produces work that is internally excellent and entirely unusable, and it costs a full worker cycle to discover.

**Consequence for the tree.** `WORKSPACE/indicator-layout-plan.md`, merged at `b1b90c4d` roughly an hour before this ruling, has a superseded central premise. It is **not** being deleted — its §2 disposition of all 24 audited marks, §7 cost table and §9 guessing list survive, as does the record of why corner badges are impossible for infantry (`ShowNever: true` at `infantry.yaml:56-57`, the only such line in the mod). The next worker adds a dated superseding banner in place, per this project's convention that a dated record is corrected visibly rather than quietly rewritten.

**Two things the next generation must not lose:**
1. **Impediment does not exist in code.** It is specified and not built, so half of what the diamond is being designed to show is a proposal. Any mockup must label which channel is shipped and which is specced, or it violates decision 08 — do not draw a mark as a substitute for a mechanic that does not work.
2. **The level-resolution discipline is the whole point.** The defect being fixed is a ten-step quantity drawn with three distinguishable states at 6×9 pixels. Every variant must declare how many levels it actually resolves at true size. "Ten colours of one glyph" was already judged to sink the old suppression pip, and the user has now proposed colour for a continuous quantity — that is the same shape of idea and must be drawn honestly rather than refused or waved through.
