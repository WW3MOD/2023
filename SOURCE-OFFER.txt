WHAT IS NOT COVERED BY THAT LICENSE
-----------------------------------

WW3MOD is a total conversion of Command & Conquer: Red Alert and loads artwork
and audio from that game's data files. Those data files are NOT part of WW3MOD,
are NOT distributed with it, and are NOT covered by the GNU GPL. They remain the
property of their respective owners. WW3MOD offers, on first launch, to download
them from a mirror of the 2008 Red Alert freeware release, or to copy them from
an original disc or digital installation you already own.

The game engine is derived from OpenRA (https://www.openra.net), also licensed
under the GNU GPL version 3. See AUTHORS for the engine's contributors.
