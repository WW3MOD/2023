defect family cannot quietly widen without the numbers being re-measured.

APPROACHMARGIN: NOT RAISED, and the reasons are tests rather than opinions.
Raising it cannot lengthen the approach the player sees. Bearing does not take
the margin, so a bigger standoff slides the birth point back along an unchanged
line and the over-the-board segment is identical to the world unit -- asserted at
every shipped map size, three homes and the full aim sweep, at 1x and 4x. The
camera cannot make up the difference: the world window is 1280x720 world px at
the default Medium setting, 1920x1080 at Far on a 1080p screen, TileSize 24, so
the widest visible half-window is 40 cells against a standoff of 90.2 cells on
the SMALLEST shipped map. And raising it brings the warhead into view LOWER, not
higher, because the altitude ramp is spread over the whole standoff. Cost of a 4x
raise, measured: +5-16% order-to-impact on every power in the mod (+1.4 s to
+7.4 s), and Sandbox click-to-detonation 6.1-30.7 s becoming 7.6-38.0 s. Nothing
gained that anyone can see.
