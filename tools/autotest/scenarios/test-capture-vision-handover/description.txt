A building that changes hands must hand its vision over with it. A USA rifleman garrisons a Neutral church, which flips the building's owner through GarrisonManager's in-place path; the scenario then reads one probe cell inside the building's vision radius and asserts both halves — the new owner gains LIVE vision (resolved band >= 2, not the explored-only floor of 1) and the previous owner drops to explored-only. A third limb changes an ordinary rifleman's owner through the World.Remove/Add path, which fires the same notification while the actor is out of the world, to prove the IsInWorld guard in AffectsMapLayer.OnOwnerChanged still stands: without it that limb does not fail, it throws.
