TEST: the bot advances to the DEFCON 3 border and holds there.

A vertical line down column 44 splits a 100x60 map between USA-bot (west, Supply
Route at x 3) and Russia (east, Supply Route at x 94). The match is pinned at
DEFCON 3 for thirty minutes, so the border never comes down during the run.

Eight tanks are placed around the bot's Supply Route. The enemy Supply Route is a
PoiAction.Pressure POI, so PoiOffensiveBotModule forms an axis against it and
orders the armour east -- straight through the border, before this change.

WHAT THE THREE ASSERTIONS SEPARATE:

  ADVANCE     At least one bot tank must reach x >= 30, i.e. it crossed most of
              the ground between its Supply Route and the line. THIS IS THE
              DISCRIMINATOR. Before BorderStagingEnabled the axis ordered to
              x 94; AttackMove has no border guard so the order was accepted,
              NearestMoveableCell failed CanReach on the far cell, the annulus
              around it was also beyond the border, and Move completed without
              moving. The tanks stayed on their spawn at x 6-9 for the whole
              match, because the axis had recorded x 94 as "ordered" and its own
              repath guard then refused to re-issue.

  NO CROSSING No bot unit is ever observed at x >= 43, the west edge of the wall
              band. This passed before the fix too -- the units could not move at
              all -- so it is an invariant rather than the discriminator. It is
              checked every tick, because a wall that is crossed and returned
              from is not a wall.

  HOLD        Over the last twenty harness-seconds, no bot unit moves more than
              two cells and the bot's cumulative order count grows by less than
              120. Together these say the advance ENDED: the axis is parked at
              the border rather than re-offering a destination it cannot reach
              every hundred-tick scan. The order counter is read through
              Test.BotOrdersQueued, which counts at ModularBot.QueueOrder -- the
              one funnel every module's order passes -- and counts BEFORE the
              arbitration gate, so a module re-offering a suppressed order still
              shows up.

RED SABOTAGE: set BorderStagingEnabled to false in rules.yaml. Expected failure
is the ADVANCE assertion: "bot never advanced: furthest tank reached x=<n>
(need x>=30); it is still parked on its Supply Route". The other two still pass,
which is exactly why ADVANCE is the one that carries the verdict.
