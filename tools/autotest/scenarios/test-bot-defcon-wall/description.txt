TEST: the bot advances to the DEFCON 3 border and holds there.

A vertical line down column 44 splits a 100x60 map between USA-bot (west, Supply
Route at x 3) and Russia (east, Supply Route at x 94). The match is pinned at
DEFCON 3 for thirty minutes, so the border never comes down during the run.

Eight tanks are placed around the bot's Supply Route. The enemy Supply Route is a
PoiAction.Pressure POI, so PoiOffensiveBotModule forms an axis against it and
orders the armour east -- straight through the border, before this change.

WHAT THE THREE ASSERTIONS SEPARATE:

  ADVANCE     At least one bot tank must reach x >= 30. This is a floor, NOT the
              discriminator — CORRECTED 2026-09-13 against the first RED run,
              which passed it. The prediction it was written from was that the
              axis would park the army on its Supply Route: the order to x 94 is
              accepted (AttackMove has no border guard), NearestMoveableCell
              fails CanReach on the far cell, the annulus around it is also
              beyond the border, Move finds no path and completes without moving
              — and the axis then records x 94 as "ordered" so its repath guard
              refuses to re-issue. Every step of that is still true of the AXIS
              ORDER. What the prediction missed is that the axis order is not the
              only thing moving these units: the fires anchors, the echelon and
              the forward-staging anchors all resolve to cells on our OWN side,
              which are perfectly reachable, and they walk the army up the map
              regardless. So the army advances either way and this assertion
              cannot tell the arms apart.

              What the border staging actually buys is that the army SETTLES.

  NO CROSSING No bot unit is ever observed at x >= 43, the west edge of the wall
              band. This passed before the fix too -- the units could not move at
              all -- so it is an invariant rather than the discriminator. It is
              checked every tick, because a wall that is crossed and returned
              from is not a wall.

  HOLD        Over the last twenty harness-seconds, no bot unit moves more than
              two cells, and PoiOffensiveBotModule queues fewer than 150 orders.
              Together these say the advance ENDED: the axis is parked at the
              border rather than re-offering a destination it cannot reach every
              hundred-tick scan. DRIFT is the predicate that discriminates -- the
              RED arm fails on it at 18 cells against a 2-cell bound -- and the
              order count is the belt to that braces.

              THE ORDER COUNT IS SCOPED TO ONE MODULE, and that is a correction
              rather than a detail. It first counted every order the bot queued
              and allowed 120; the window carried 554 and the assertion failed
              while blaming the axis, which was innocent. debug.log for that run
              holds exactly ONE [exp-offense] order line across the window's five
              evaluations -- the staging clamp and the repath guard were working.
              The 554 were the rest of the bot: 43 [composition] production lines
              on a map where its unit count went 8 to 43 mid-run, the supply and
              capture lanes, and 20 [exp-transport] delivery-move-reissued lines
              from two carriers stuck against the border. A total can only ever
              measure the bot's overall busyness; the question here is about one
              module, so it asks about one module.

RED SABOTAGE: set BorderStagingEnabled to false in rules.yaml. MEASURED
(run 260913_233651): it fails on DRIFT — "abrams drifted 18 cells during the hold
window (max 2)" — and passes ADVANCE. That is the honest discriminator and it is
the one to read: without the staging clamp the axis destination is a cell the
army can never reach, so the army never arrives anywhere, never settles, and is
still being shoved around by the subsidiary anchors when the window closes.

KNOWN OPEN DEFECT THIS SCENARIO NO LONGER FAILS ON, stated so a green run is not
read as more than it is. MountedTransportBotModule picks infantry drop-offs with
no regard for the border: in the run above two carriers sat at x 1 and x 3 with a
drop cell at 49,28, well beyond the band, and re-issued the delivery Move every
50 ticks for the rest of the match without moving. Mobile.ResolveOrder refuses a
Move across the border (Mobile.cs:1111-1115) and the module is never told, so the
loop is permanent and the passengers are never delivered. It is the same defect
class BorderStagingEnabled fixes for the offensive module, in a module that was
not in that change's scope and has no scenario of its own. This file's assertions
are deliberately scoped away from it rather than silently absorbing it.
