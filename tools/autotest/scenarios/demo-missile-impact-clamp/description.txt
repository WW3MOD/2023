Demo: a sub-tick-smoothed missile now stops dead on its target instead of gliding through it. Six passes, each firing TWO Kinzhals at once at bare aim cells 44,51 and 47,47 - five cells apart, perpendicular to the inbound heading, both 88-89 cells from Russia's HomeLocation so they arrive within a tick of each other. One is the shipped kinzhalmissile (SubTickMotionSmoothing plus WithHypersonicPlasma); the other is kinzhalmissileraw, the same actor with the smoothing trait removed and nothing else changed. THE RAW ONE IS THE REFERENCE, not the other half of a comparison: a missile drawn only at simulated positions cannot overshoot, so wherever it sits when its blast appears is where a missile is supposed to be when it goes off. WHAT TO LOOK AT is the last quarter-second only. BallisticMissileFly places the missile ON the target and spends one more tick finishing before the queued Kill lands, and through that one ~60 ms interval the smoothing trait still held the arrival step - up to 2400 wdist, ~56 px at 100% zoom - with no way to know the simulation was done moving it. So the sprite swept a full step past the impact point and the explosion went off behind it. Fixed by clamping the prediction to the distance still left to travel. PASS: the smoothed missile comes to a dead stop on its aim point exactly as the raw one does, and both blasts appear where their missiles are standing. FAIL: the smoothed missile glides about two cells past its aim point after arriving and the blast appears behind it. SECOND SUBJECT, same clamp: the shipped Kinzhal's 480-wdist plasma bloom is drawn AHEAD of the nose and used to sit ~11 px past the crater point for that same tick; it now shortens into the body sample by sample and is gone at contact. The orange wake behind the missile is deliberately unclamped and should be unchanged. The tracks swap sides every pass so a difference cannot be blamed on where it happened on screen. Order ticks 30 / 130 / 230 / 330 / 430 / 530; each pair enters +15 far off screen, comes into frame ~+60 already in the terminal dive, impacts ~+74. Camera fixed at the midpoint of the two aim points and never moves; the demo zooms itself to 3x at WorldLoaded because the artefact lasts four frames at 60 fps. DO NOT PAUSE TO COMPARE: smoothing is deliberately inert while the world is paused, so both missiles sit on identical true positions in a paused frame - that is correct, not the fix working. Both powers keep recharging on a 60-tick interval afterwards, so the viewer can fire either from the bin.
