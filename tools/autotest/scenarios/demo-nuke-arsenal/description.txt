Demo: all six nuclear-arsenal warheads fired at the same cell in ascending yield - B61-12 at 0.3 kt, B61-12 at 50 kt, W76-1, RS-28 Sarmat, B83-1 and Tsar Bomba. 142 identical target sites in thirteen rings from 3 to 82 cells, so each shot kills a ring the previous one could not reach. Eight frames: an opening frame, one per detonation, and an aftermath. Nothing is asserted; it ends on Test.Skip. The camera frames itself - Camera.Zoom is set to MinZoom in WorldLoaded, so the old "ZOOM OUT" instruction is gone. Trees are indestructible mod-wide and will not change; read the structures and the infantry.

TWO SEATS FIRE, NOT ONE (fixed 2026-09-21). This demo could not fire two of its six warheads and had not been able to since the nuclear powers were faction-tiered. MissileStrikePower@Sarmat and @TsarBomba each carry Prerequisites: powers.event, player.russia, and the sandbox lobby option this scenario locks on grants powers.event to everybody but never grants player.russia - ProvidesPrerequisite@Russia is Factions: russia. An America seat was one prerequisite short on exactly those two. The failure was silent at every layer: the proxy is filtered out of BuildableItems, ProductionQueue.ResolveOrder drops the order and returns, and Lua's Player.Build returns true regardless - so EnsurePower reported 'buying' forever rather than the 'refused' that would have named the cause. The fix is a seat: USA fires shots 1/2/3/5 and RUSSIA fires the Sarmat and the Tsar Bomba, from the bare map combatant that was already on this map. The B83 stays on USA because player.america was removed from it on 2026-09-20, leaving it event-tier and unowned.

THE SCHEDULE IS EVENT-DRIVEN AND EACH DETONATION IS OBSERVED. The old absolute tick table (impacts at 161 / 351 / 666 / 1020 / 1632 / 2391) was stale twice over - it counted the pre-b69681d2 map-edge flight, and it counted a MissileDelay that sandbox drops (SandboxRemovesLaunchDelay defaults true, so the six MissileDelay: 60 overrides in rules.yaml resolve to 0). Rather than re-derive a table the next engine change would stale again, the script now enumerates the strike's missile actors every tick and treats the count returning to zero as the detonation. Shot N+1 is ordered a settle after shot N's observed impact, so nothing overlaps whatever the flight times turn out to be. lua.log carries one ARSENAL DETONATION line per warhead with its name, tick, observed warhead count and flight time; six of those lines is the demo working.

SHOTS 4 AND 5 FLY SIX WARHEADS EACH ON THIS MAP, and the ring attribution in map.yaml's table no longer holds for them. AimPoints is deliberately absent from @Sarmat and @B83; MissileStrikePower.AimPointsFor overrides it for anything in the game-ender band and returns DoomsdayStrike.PackageSize, which on these Bounds (15876 playable cells / 2400) clamps to 6. Six 71-cell B83 bursts on its own 35-cell fallback ring reach about 106 cells from ground zero - past ring 82, and so past everything the Tsar Bomba was staged to be the first to reach. The Tsar is above the ladder's sandbox-only ceiling, is therefore not a game-ender, and still flies one bomb. The observed count is printed with every detonation so a viewer reads the real number.
