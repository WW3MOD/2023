AUTOTEST: cohesion sticky-slot leash (Phase 4 v1)

Verifies CohesionSlotMemory: a unit nudged off its assigned slot walks
back when the path is free again.

Setup: 4 e3.america in a vertical column at (20, 14..17). Trees flank
their north and south to force a passing unit through. A 5th unit
(Bumper) is ordered to march from west to east across the column.

The grouped move is issued at (20, 15) so the bidder assigns each unit
a slot equal to its starting position (Open intent, box formation
centered on click). Then Bumper moves east; one or more of A1..A4 get
nudged aside; CohesionSlotMemory queues a return Move; after a wait the
column is reformed.

Verdict: pass if all 4 units end up at their initial (= assigned-slot)
cells.
