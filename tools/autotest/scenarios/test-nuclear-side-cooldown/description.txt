One launch puts the WHOLE SIDE on one cooldown -- every player on it, at every band -- and stops
at the team line. Each faction also holds only its own ladder.

A 2v1 with an America player and a Russia player on ONE team, against a Russia opponent on another.
USA fires its 0.3 kt B61 and three things must be true 30 ticks later:

  USA's own B61      charging:   the firer is on its side's cooldown
  Volga's 9M729      charging:   a DIFFERENT weapon, on a DIFFERENT player, same side
  Enemy's 9M729      ready       the identical weapon, on Team 2, untouched

The third reading is what makes the second one evidence. On 2026-09-14 this scenario passed while
all three combatants were keyed onto ONE side -- debug.log read `Volga(1), Enemy(1), USA(1)` -- so
"the teammate went on cooldown" was trivially true of everybody on the board and nothing in the run
could tell that apart from the feature working.

Then the whole side comes back at once (t480), because a cooldown that never expired would be a
side permanently disarmed by its own first shot.

The faction lock is asserted first, as an absence: USA's 9M729 and Volga's B61 must both read
`hidden`. `powers-sandbox` is locked OFF in rules.yaml, because it hands every player all three
tiers and would make that assertion vacuous.

Renamed from test-nuclear-band-regen at exchange v2 (2026-09-15). Under v1 the rule was "one shot
spends the BAND, for the side"; it is now "one shot spends the SIDE", so the same three readings
pin a wider rule and the cooldown field is KilotonCooldownTicks rather than KilotonRegenTicks.
