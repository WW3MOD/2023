The fix clamps the position into Map.Bounds, passes FloatingText's ignoreVisibility, and lengthens the rise from 30 ticks to 75 (1.8s to 4.5s at Timestep 60).

MUST be run with AUTOTEST_EXTRA_ARGS="Test.KeepRenderPlayer=true", AND THE REASON IS NOT THE USUAL ONE. The usual reason is that TestModeLogic.cs:30-31 nulls World.RenderPlayer for every autotest holding a real player slot, so fog disappears and the frame stops being a picture of what a player sees. That applies here but is the weaker half. The stronger half is that a null RenderPlayer makes ONE OF THE TWO THINGS THE FIX CHANGED UNFALSIFIABLE: FloatingText's own gate is `!ignoreVisibility && (FogObscures || ShroudObscures)` (FloatingText.cs:66) and World.FogObscures/ShroudObscures short-circuit to false the instant RenderPlayer is null (World.cs:109-115), so the text draws whether or not the ignoreVisibility bypass is passed at all -- and DoSell's own `self.Owner.IsAlliedWith(self.World.RenderPlayer)` gate is vacuous too, because Player.RelationshipWith returns Ally for a null argument outright (Player.cs:254-255). A run without the flag therefore photographs a code path THAT CANNOT FAIL and comes back with a perfectly plausible picture of two refund ticks. That is the one failure mode here that would be read as evidence.

SO THE RUN REFUSES TO PRODUCE A VERDICT WHEN THE FLAG IS MISSING. FarBox is a Russia pillbox at 60,30 with all ten Vision layers stripped (it inherits PBOX, which inherits ^Defense -> ^StandardVision, structures-defenses.yaml:5), ~55 cells from either subject and well past that ladder's 32-cell outermost rung, on a map with ExploredMapCheckboxEnabled FALSE and locked -- so USA has never looked at it and its cell reads visibility 0. Test.IsMouseTargetable asks MouseTargetVisibility.IsRevealed, whose actorIsVisible term is !World.FogObscures(a) and which ANDs the whole expression with it: false under a live RenderPlayer, true under a null one. Targetable therefore means null RenderPlayer and nothing else. Both preconditions are asserted before anything is pressed -- the cell must read 0 AND the box must be untargetable -- so a stray vision source invalidating the probe reports as PREMISE BROKEN rather than being silently absorbed. This is the same probe test-unscouted-building-hidden already relies on.

GEOMETRY, AND WHY THE FRAME CAN SEPARATE THREE OUTCOMES RATHER THAN TWO. Bounds are 1,1,64,32, so Map.Bounds is Left=1 Top=1 Right=65 Bottom=33 with Right and Bottom EXCLUSIVE and the last legal west column is x=1. Both subjects exit WEST, and that is derived rather than hoped for: RotateToEdge.ChooseEdgeCell takes the ground branch, whose searchOrigin is self.Location (no SpawnArea actor exists on this map, so FindClosestSpawnAreaForOwner returns null and the fallback applies), and Map.ChooseClosestMatchingEdgeCell sorts Map.AllEdgeCells -- the perimeter of BOUNDS, built by UpdateEdgeCells -- by straight-line distance. From Payer at 6,14 the nearest perimeter cell is 1,14 at 5 cells against 6,1 at 13; from Pauper at 6,19 it is 1,19 at 5 against 6,32 at 13. West wins by more than 2x in both cases, so no pathing wobble can flip it, and `make nav-guard --scenarios` reports 2048 reachable cells in ONE region for every ground locomotor including heavytracked, so ChooseEdgeCell's CanReach predicate cannot reject the west perimeter. The camera is centred on cell 8 at pinned zoom 2.0, putting roughly ten cells of off-map black to the LEFT of column x=1 in frame. That is what buys the three-way read: a tick ON column x=1 just inside the boundary means the clamp works; a tick roughly two cells out IN THE BLACK VOID means the clamp is gone but the visibility bypass is holding; NO tick at all means suppression, the original bug. "Absent" and "drawn where nobody can see it" are different failures with different fixes and a capture that could not separate them would be worth very little.

THE VERDICT SEPARATES "no text" FROM "no sale", which the picture alone cannot. The run records the USA cash delta at the tick each subject is observed dead, so if the PNG shows nothing while the verdict reports money credited, the sale happened and the INDICATOR is what is broken; if no credit was recorded either, nothing evacuated and the frame is not about the indicator at all. It also carries the last in-world cell of each subject and the cell EvacRefundTextMath.ClampToBounds would map it to, so the reader has the predicted text position in result.json without opening a log. The clamp is mirrored in Lua purely to PRINT that prediction and never feeds back into the engine; doing it in CPos space is legitimate because this is a rectangular TEMPERAT map where the MPos the engine clamps in and the CPos read here are the same numbers.

TIMING, AND THE ONE CLOCK. The two evacuations are ordered 20 ticks apart, not together, for two load-bearing reasons: per-tick cash deltas would merge into one unattributable number if both sales landed on the same tick, and two texts spawned simultaneously would be indistinguishable in frame if either failed to draw. The ACHIEVED gap is not the ordered one -- the subjects pick different edge cells and drive different distances, and run 260901_225727_p29763 measured 33 -- so nothing here assumes it. The shot is armed 10 ticks after the second sale and the verdict 25 after that, because the capture is ONE FRAME LATE (Test.Screenshot arms a capture and the pixels are sampled at the end of the NEXT RenderTick) so nothing may touch the world in the same closure. Ten rather than the original fifteen because the OLDER text's age at the shutter is gap + ShotDelayTicks + 1, and that delay is the only term in the sum the scenario controls.

EVERY TICK THE VERDICT REPORTS IS DateTime.GameTime, THE SAME COUNTER result.json's screenshots[].tick CARRIES (Context.World.WorldTick, stamped in TestGlobal.cs:102; exposed to Lua at DateTimeGlobal.cs:39). This is not tidiness, it is the correction of the one thing that has already misled a reader of this instrument. Poll used to count its own iterations from 1 and the first runs at WorldTick 29, so the first run's verdict said "sold at tick 151" against a screenshot saying "tick 227" -- a 28-tick offset that made the older text look 76/75 ticks old, i.e. expired one tick before the shutter, and so supplied a confident and wrong explanation for an absence that had not occurred. THE VERDICT NOW STATES EACH TEXT'S AGE AT THE SHUTTER OUTRIGHT, as "N/75 ticks -- ALIVE" or "-- EXPIRED", because an absent text has exactly two causes (it did not draw, or it had already died) and they are indistinguishable in the PNG. An EXPIRED reading is also pushed into the setup-suspect list, which downgrades the frame to "not evidence". That age check replaced a gap-derived one that compared the gap against TickLifetime directly and so could not fire until 75 -- the older text actually dies at gap + ShotDelayTicks + 1, which at the then-current delay of 15 is gap >= 59 -- so the old form was silent for every gap from 59 to 74 while the frame it was blessing had already lost one of its two arms.
