Demo: drives a DEFCON Escalation match through all three levels on the shipped River Zeta map so the in-game readout can be seen. Clocks are compressed to 300 ticks (18 s) and the nuclear release gate to 1200 (72 s) - a capture has only ~200 s of wall clock and has to pass three levels plus the gate. Reaching DEFCON 1 requires a kill by enemy action, so the script spawns an Abrams and an enemy truck five cells apart and ORDERS the attack at t=360; autonomous fire is suppressed at DEFCON 2 by the very rule being demonstrated, so nothing happens unless it is ordered. Five frames: DEFCON 3 with its clock, the 3-to-2 banner, DEFCON 2 with the em dash and the trigger line, the 2-to-1 banner, and DEFCON 1 with the nuclear release countdown. The last two are timed off Trigger.OnKilled rather than a tick number, because the kill tick is the one thing here that cannot be predicted. Nothing is asserted.
