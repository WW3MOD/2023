Demo: drives a DEFCON Escalation match through all three levels and then through the nuclear exchange, on the shipped River Zeta map, so the in-game readout can be seen. Clocks are compressed to 300 ticks (18 s), the nuclear release gate to 1200 (72 s), and the 1 kt SIDE COOLDOWN to 900 ticks so its recovery lands inside the run. Reaching DEFCON 1 requires a kill by enemy action, so the script spawns an Abrams and an enemy truck five cells apart and ORDERS the attack at t=360; autonomous fire is suppressed at DEFCON 2 by the very rule being demonstrated, so nothing happens unless it is ordered. NINE frames. 01-05 are the strip: DEFCON 3 with its clock, the 3-to-2 banner, DEFCON 2 with the em dash and the trigger line, the 2-to-1 banner, and DEFCON 1 with the gate still shut and both ledger rows dark. 06-09 are the ledger and the ESCALATED moment: the NUCLEAR RELEASE banner with 1 kt lit and READY on both rows, the ESCALATED banner with the viewer's row now lit to 20 kt while the firer's row dims and starts counting, the asymmetric mid-cooldown state, and the recovery. RUSSIA fires the 1 kt, not USA, because any launch raises the OTHER side's level and USA is the playable slot - a shot from USA would put the banner on a screen no capture can see. Everything after the kill hangs off Trigger.OnKilled rather than a tick number, because the kill tick is the one thing here that cannot be predicted. Nothing is asserted; it ends on Test.Skip.
