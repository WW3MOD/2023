ASSERTS that a shift-queued evacuation commits its map-edge destination when the order is QUEUED, not when the activity becomes current -- and produces one UNGRADED PNG beside that assertion, because the assertion cannot see the renderer. Guards adfb0f2f, which moved RotateToEdge.ChooseEdgeCell out of OnFirstRun and into the constructor. edgeCell is the only input to RotateToEdge.TargetLineNodes, and it was null until the activity ran, so the queued evacuation leg drew no waypoint line at all until the unit reached the waypoint before it -- the user's "it shows up only at the last waypoint". The feature IS the immediacy: the leg must be on screen while the unit is still walking leg one.

WHAT A PASS MEANS, AND WHAT IT DOES NOT. A PASS means that while the tank is still on its first leg, Test.GetTargetLineCells reports four nodes -- the three ordered waypoints then the edge cell resolved from the tank's ISSUE-TIME position. A PASS DOES NOT MEAN THE LINE IS ON SCREEN and must never be read that way: Test.GetTargetLineCells walks the activity queue and never consults DrawLineToTarget.ShouldRender, the selection, the TargetLines setting or the renderer, every one of which can be broken with this assertion still green. The Test.Pass note says so in the verdict itself so it reaches result.json. The only evidence about rendering is the PNG, and the PNG is ungraded -- a human reads it. Both halves are needed and neither substitutes for the other.

WHY NO EXISTING CAPTURE COULD SHOW THIS. test-evac-queued-after-waypoints does screenshot a queued evacuation and its PNG can never show a target line whatever the code does, for two independent reasons in DrawLineToTarget: IRenderAnnotationsWhenSelected runs only for a SELECTED actor and by the time that scenario shoots the selection has long since moved to another unit; and ShouldRender (DrawLineToTarget.cs:96) needs `force || Game.RunTime <= lifetime || HasAutomaticNode`, where `force` is a physically-held Shift key that no script can arrange and HasAutomaticNode is false because a player-issued Move is not an automatic order -- leaving `lifetime`, which is Game.RunTime + 2400 ms and is RE-ARMED ONLY BY INotifySelected.Selected. So a capture of this feature must hold the selection and keep re-arming that window through the moment of the shot. Test.SelectActors is what does it: Selection.Combine fires INotifySelected.Selected across its whole newSelection list unconditionally, whether or not the actor was already selected (Selection.cs:120-123), so re-selecting the same tank re-arms lifetime every time. The loop runs every 3 ticks from tick 16 to tick 60.

THE 2400 ms IS REAL TIME, NOT TICKS, AND IT CUTS THE SAFE WAY. At the mod's Timestep of 60 ms that window is about 40 ticks, so re-selecting every 3 ticks is roughly 180 ms -- a 13x margin. The margin only erodes if the game runs SLOWER than nominal, and it would take 480 ms per tick (an eight-fold slowdown) to break it; a game running FASTER makes the margin larger. So the standing trap that TestHarness.TicksPerSecond is 25 while the mod actually runs at 16.67 ticks/s cannot bite here in the dangerous direction. THE SELECTION LOOP DELIBERATELY RUNS THROUGH THE CAPTURE, which inverts the usual rule that nothing may touch the world between arming a shot and the pixels being sampled: the only state the loop changes is "the subject is selected and its order-line window is open", which is exactly the state being photographed, and stopping it first would risk photographing the line going dark.

DO NOT PASS Test.KeepRenderPlayer=true. Unlike test-evac-refund-indicator, which needs it, nothing here reads World.RenderPlayer -- DrawLineToTarget.ShouldRender gates on self.Owner.IsAlliedWith(self.World.LocalPlayer) (DrawLineToTarget.cs:80) and LineTargetExts.ShowTargetLines on self.Owner == self.World.LocalPlayer, and LocalPlayer is untouched by TestModeLogic. If passing the flag changes the outcome, that is itself a finding.

GEOMETRY, AND WHY THE EVAC LEG DOUBLES BACK ACROSS THE WHOLE MAP. Runner starts at 8,16 with three eastward waypoints (18,16 / 28,16 / 38,16) and then a Shift-queued Evacuate. ChooseEdgeCell's ground branch passes self.Location as the search origin -- there is no SpawnArea actor on this map so FindClosestSpawnAreaForOwner returns null and the fallback applies -- into Map.ChooseClosestMatchingEdgeCell, which sorts Map.AllEdgeCells (the perimeter of Bounds 1,1,64,32) by straight-line distance. Because the resolution now happens in the CONSTRUCTOR, self.Location is 8,16 and the nearest perimeter cell is 1,16 at 7 cells, beating 8,1 at 15 and 8,32 at 16. THE EVAC LEG THEREFORE RUNS 37 CELLS FROM THE LAST WAYPOINT AT 38,16 BACK WEST TO 1,16, overlaying the white move legs along row 16 and continuing past the tank to the boundary. That doubling-back is correct and is the discriminator: resolved LATE, from 38,16 where the tank would be standing when the activity became current, the nearest perimeter cell is 38,1 at 15 cells against 64,16 at 26 and 1,16 at 37 -- so a late resolution points NORTH. Three geometrically separated outcomes: a leg to 1,16 is the fix; a leg to 38,1 is a late resolution; no leg at all is the pre-fix behaviour, since TargetLineNodes yields nothing while edgeCell is null. `make nav-guard --scenarios` reports 2048 reachable cells in ONE region for every ground locomotor, so ChooseEdgeCell's CanReach predicate cannot reject the west perimeter and "no leg" cannot mean "no reachable edge".

THE FAILURE MESSAGES NAME THE CAUSE, AND ONE OUTCOME IS DELIBERATELY A SKIP RATHER THAN A FAIL. Three move waypoints and no fourth node is reported as THE BUG by name. A fourth node at 38,1 is reported as a late resolution, at 8,16 as an empty edge search. Zero nodes is reported as "the orders were never accepted" with the activity chain attached. But if the tank has ALREADY EATEN the first waypoint by the assertion tick, the head node is legitimately gone and the remaining three would be indistinguishable IN COUNT from the three a pre-fix build produces -- so that case checks the tank's actual position and SKIPS with "lower AssertTick" rather than issuing a false accusation. Checking the node CONTENTS and not merely the count is what makes that separation possible at all. The run also skips rather than grading if the Shift+E press was not consumed by the command bar (that is a command-bar regression, and test-evac-queued-after-waypoints is the scenario that grades it) and if the selection did not hold exactly one actor at the moment of the capture (in which case IRenderAnnotationsWhenSelected may not have run and the PNG cannot be read as evidence about rendering).

WHAT THE READER LOOKS FOR IN THE PNG: one tank near the left, still short of x=18, with three WHITE move legs running east along row 16 and one AMBER/GOLD leg (ARGB 180,255,200,80, DrawLineToTarget's EvacuateLineColor) running from 38,16 back west to the map boundary at 1,16. The amber leg being present at all, while the tank is still on leg one, is the whole feature. Three white legs and no amber one means the line is not RENDERING even though the node exists -- a different failure from the one the assertion grades, and the reason the capture is worth a slot.
