TEST: firing raises the enemy's level and puts your whole side on cooldown.

Two players on a 66x34 map, DEFCON Escalation, starting at DEFCON 1 with the
first-warheads clock compressed to ten ticks and the four side cooldowns
compressed to 900/700/1400/1000 ticks against a shipped 5000/7000/9000/12000
(5/7/9/12 minutes, the 2026-09-15 user ruling). The ESCALATION DELAY is NOT
compressed and is not overridden: this run uses the shipped
EscalationDelayTicks of 50 and the shipped missile flights. Nobody has any
money.

This is the nuclear exchange v2 model end to end: a side has a LEVEL (the
highest band it may fire, which never falls) and a COOLDOWN (one clock, shared
by every player on that side and by every band they hold). Firing band b sets
the firer's own cooldown and raises every ENEMY side to max(level, b + 1). The
firer's own level never moves.

Every reading is taken off the support power bin -- the same predicate that
decides whether a player sees a clickable cameo.

RETIMED FOR THE IMPACT-DEFERRED ESCALATION (2026-09-19). This scenario used to
set `EscalationDelayTicks: -1` in rules.yaml, which restored the pre-2026-09-16
timing where a victim's level rose on the tick the enemy clicked; it did so
because the phase schedule was a column of constants that fired and then read a
level 65 ticks later. The override is gone. Every phase from the first launch on
hangs off an event the run OBSERVES: the explosion (a delta on
Test.GetImpactEffectCount, snapshotted at the order) and the escalation (the
victim's new band leaving HIDDEN). Only t90 and t120 are still chosen. So the
tick numbers below are gone with the constants -- each phase is named by what it
waits for.

Two assertions per launch come out of that, and they are the coverage this
scenario adds; nothing in the tree ran the deferral end to end before.

  (a) the victim's new band stays HIDDEN on every tick between the launch and
      the explosion. RED the instant an escalation is applied at the click --
      which is exactly what the deleted override restored.
  (b) and is drawn within EscalationDelayTicks (50) plus a 40-tick grant
      allowance of that explosion. RED if the deferral never fires at all: a
      dropped pending record, an impact MissileStrikePower never reported. (a)
      cannot see that -- a level that never rises never rises early either.

  PHASE A (t90)     Release has opened. BOTH sides' 1 kt power reads READY and
                    nothing above it is visible on either side, including the
                    game-enders. Release is parity, not a provocation.

  PHASE B (t120)    USA fires its 0.3 kt B61 at empty ground. Nothing is aimed
                    at anything: this model escalates the other side wherever the
                    warhead lands, with no damage attribution at all.

  PHASE B2 (t160)   THE SIDE COOLDOWN, READ OFF THE LAUNCH. USA's own 1 kt reads
                    CHARGING with about 860 ticks left -- 900 minus the 40 since
                    the shot, which is the KilotonCooldownTicks entry and not one
                    of the other three -- and Russia's 1 kt reads READY. The
                    cooldown belongs to the side that fired and is charged the
                    instant the button is pressed, minutes before anything lands.

  PHASE C           THE RATCHET, twelve ticks after Russia's 20 kt was drawn.
  (rise 1 + 12)     It reads READY -- one band above what it was hit with, loaded,
                    with no purchase possible -- and USA's own 20 kt is still
                    HIDDEN. Under the shared pressure ladder this replaced, both
                    would have opened together.

  PHASE C2          Russia answers at 20 kt.
  (phase C + 10)

  PHASE C3          AND RUSSIA'S WHOLE ARSENAL WENT DOWN WITH IT, on the 20 kt
  (C2 + 40)         entry: ~660 ticks left of 700. Its 1 kt is CHARGING on the
                    same clock -- which is v1's per-band regeneration failing:
                    under v1 one shot muted one band and left the rest up.

  PHASE D           THE SHARPEST ASSERTION IN THE FILE. USA's brand-new 50 kt
  (rise 2 + 12)     and 20 kt are DRAWN and NOT FIREABLE: the level is real and
                    the cooldown is too, and they are separate gates. Being shot
                    at while reloading is the NORMAL case in this model, so a
                    build whose level rise zeroes the timer is broken in most
                    matches and passes every other test in the tree.

                    USA IS RELOADING FROM ITS OWN t120 SHOT, the natural shape of
                    this model, and KilotonCooldownTicks (900) is sized to
                    outlast the whole flight-plus-delay that separates that shot
                    from Russia's reply landing. The phase checks that setup
                    FIRST and reports "SCENARIO SETUP" naming the constant if it
                    no longer holds, because a victim that is off cooldown reads
                    exactly like a build with a free-shot bug and is not one.

  PHASE E           USA's whole arsenal comes back at once, 940 ticks after its
  (t120 + 940)      shot -- its 1 kt, and both bands it gained WHILE reloading.

                    THE SECOND CLAIM THIS PHASE USED TO MAKE HAS MOVED. It read
                    at a tick where USA's 1 kt cooldown had expired and Russia's
                    20 kt one had not, separating two of the four constants by
                    inference; that needed two launches inside one cooldown of
                    each other, which a deferred escalation makes unreliable. The
                    claim is now made directly and four times -- phases B2, C3,
                    F2 and G3 each read the applied cooldown as a NUMBER and
                    compare it against that band's entry, within six ticks. That
                    is also why the four values are no longer in ascending order.

  PHASE F (E+10)    USA fires 50 kt.
  PHASE F2 (F+40)   The 50 kt entry, read as a number: ~1360 of 1400, with its
                    1 kt and 20 kt on the same clock.
  PHASE F3          Russia reaches level 4 -- the band whose reply is the ender.
  (rise 3 + 12)

  PHASE G (F3+10)   Russia fires 100 kt.
  PHASE G3 (G+40)   The 100 kt entry, read as a number: ~960 of 1000.

  PHASE G2          USA's game-ender is DRAWN and NOT FIREABLE, because USA is
  (rise 4 + 12)     still reloading from its own 50 kt -- phase D's situation at
                    the top rung, with the same SCENARIO SETUP guard in front of
                    it and FiftyKilotonCooldownTicks (1400) sized for it.
                    Same rule through a DIFFERENT code path: the top rung is
                    armed by NuclearExchange.ArmableAtTopRung, which overrides
                    the `powers.event` prerequisite no faction provides, and a
                    fix there that reached for MakeReady without re-applying the
                    cooldown would open the match-ender early.

                    NATIONAL ENDER ONLY: USA sees its B83 and never Russia's
                    Sarmat, and Russia -- which FIRED the 100 kt -- holds no
                    ender at all.

  PHASE H           The game-ender comes up when the side cooldown ends.
  (F's shot + 1440)

  PHASE I (H+10)    And the order is ACCEPTED. A drawn cameo and a fireable
                    weapon are two claims; only the second one reaches
                    DoomsdayStrike.BeginFinalExchange. The ENDING itself is not
                    asserted -- BeginFinalExchange returns immediately without
                    RunInTestMode, and with the salvo live it would resolve the
                    match while this poller is still writing.
                    demo-doomsday-deadhand is where the ending is the subject.

                    A GAME-ENDER ESCALATES IMMEDIATELY and that is not
                    configurable (NuclearExchange.ReportNuclearRelease): the
                    match is ending and there is no correlation left to see. So
                    the shot in phase I is outside everything above and nothing
                    reads it.

The run is about 2520 ticks (~2.5 min at 1x), against ~1000 before. Only ~480
of the extra is flight time -- MEASURED at 110 ticks for the B61Low and 83 for
the Iskander, because PowersSandboxCheckboxEnabled drops MissileDelay. The rest
is the two full cooldown waits phases E and H are, which grew with the cooldowns
that phases D and G2 need.

GREEN means the ladder climbed one step per exchange, the firer never climbed
itself, no band was ever fireable inside its side's cooldown, and every level
rise landed AFTER the explosion that caused it and within a few seconds of it.
