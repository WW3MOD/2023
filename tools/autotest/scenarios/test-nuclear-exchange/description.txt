TEST: firing arms the other side, and the retaliation window lapses.

Two players on a 66x34 map, DEFCON Escalation, starting at DEFCON 1 with the
first-warheads clock compressed to ten ticks and the retaliation window set to
its shortest shipped value, one minute. Nobody has any money.

Four readings, all taken off the support power bin -- the same predicate that
decides whether a player sees a clickable cameo:

  PHASE A (t90)   Release has opened. BOTH sides' 1 kt power must read READY and
                  NEITHER side's 20 kt power may be visible. Release is parity,
                  not a provocation.

  PHASE B (t120)  USA fires its 0.3 kt B61 at empty ground. Nothing is aimed at
                  anything: this model arms the other side wherever the warhead
                  lands, with no damage attribution at all.

  PHASE C (t180)  THE ASSERTION THE SCENARIO EXISTS FOR. Russia's 20 kt power
                  must now read READY -- one band above what it was hit with,
                  loaded, with no purchase possible -- and USA's own 20 kt power
                  must still be HIDDEN. Under the shared pressure ladder this
                  replaced, both would have opened together.

  PHASE D (t1240) One minute after the shot, Russia's 20 kt is HIDDEN again and
                  Russia's 1 kt is still READY. The window expires; the parity
                  does not.

WHY 'ready' IS THE RIGHT TOKEN AND NOT 'permitted'. Every nuclear power in the
mod is RequiresPurchase, so being allowed a band only puts it in the shop. With
DefaultCash: 0 nobody can buy anything, so READY can only mean the exchange
loaded the warhead itself.

PASS: all four phases read as above.
FAIL: the verdict names which reading went wrong, with every power's state at
the end of the run and the result of the launch order.
