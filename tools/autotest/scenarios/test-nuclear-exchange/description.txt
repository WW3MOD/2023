TEST: firing raises the enemy's level and puts your whole side on cooldown.

Two players on a 66x34 map, DEFCON Escalation, starting at DEFCON 1 with the
first-warheads clock compressed to ten ticks and the four side cooldowns
compressed to 300/320/340/360 ticks against a shipped 5000/7000/9000/12000
(5/7/9/12 minutes, the 2026-09-15 user ruling). Nobody has any money.

This is the nuclear exchange v2 model end to end: a side has a LEVEL (the
highest band it may fire, which never falls) and a COOLDOWN (one clock, shared
by every player on that side and by every band they hold). Firing band b sets
the firer's own cooldown and raises every ENEMY side to max(level, b + 1). The
firer's own level never moves.

Every reading is taken off the support power bin -- the same predicate that
decides whether a player sees a clickable cameo.

  PHASE A (t90)     Release has opened. BOTH sides' 1 kt power reads READY and
                    nothing above it is visible on either side, including the
                    game-enders. Release is parity, not a provocation.

  PHASE B (t120)    USA fires its 0.3 kt B61 at empty ground. Nothing is aimed
                    at anything: this model escalates the other side wherever the
                    warhead lands, with no damage attribution at all.

  PHASE C (t185)    THE RATCHET. Russia's 20 kt reads READY -- one band above
                    what it was hit with, loaded, with no purchase possible --
                    and USA's own 20 kt is still HIDDEN. Under the shared
                    pressure ladder this replaced, both would have opened
                    together.

  PHASE C also      THE SIDE COOLDOWN. USA's own 1 kt reads CHARGING, and
                    Russia's 1 kt reads READY. The cooldown belongs to the side
                    that fired and to nobody else.

  PHASE C2 (t200)   Russia answers at 20 kt. USA goes to level 3 -- while USA is
                    still reloading from its own shot.

  PHASE D (t265)    THE SHARPEST ASSERTION IN THE FILE. USA's brand-new 50 kt
                    and 20 kt are DRAWN and NOT FIREABLE: the level is real and
                    the cooldown is too, and they are separate gates. Being shot
                    at while reloading is the NORMAL case in this model, so a
                    build whose level rise zeroes the timer is broken in most
                    matches and passes every other test in the tree.

                    Russia's 1 kt is CHARGING here as well, off a 20 kt shot --
                    which is v1's per-band regeneration failing: under v1 one
                    shot muted one band and left the rest of the arsenal up.

  PHASE E (t450)    USA's whole arsenal comes back at once, 330 ticks after its
                    shot. Russia is STILL reloading, because a 20 kt costs 320
                    ticks and it fired 80 ticks later -- which is what proves the
                    table is indexed by the band that was FIRED.

  PHASE F (t560)    USA fires 50 kt; Russia reaches level 4 (t625).

  PHASE G (t640)    Russia fires 100 kt; USA reaches level 5, END.

  PHASE G2 (t705)   USA's game-ender is DRAWN and NOT FIREABLE, because USA is
                    still reloading from its 50 kt. Same rule as phase D through
                    a DIFFERENT code path: the top rung is armed by
                    NuclearExchange.ArmableAtTopRung, which overrides the
                    `powers.event` prerequisite no faction provides, and a fix
                    there that reached for MakeReady without re-applying the
                    cooldown would open the match-ender early.

                    NATIONAL ENDER ONLY: USA sees its B83 and never Russia's
                    Sarmat, and Russia -- which FIRED the 100 kt -- holds no
                    ender at all.

  PHASE H (t970)    The game-ender comes up when the side cooldown ends.

  PHASE I (t980)    And the order is ACCEPTED. A drawn cameo and a fireable
                    weapon are two claims; only the second one reaches
                    DoomsdayStrike.BeginFinalExchange. The ENDING itself is not
                    asserted -- BeginFinalExchange returns immediately without
                    RunInTestMode, and with the salvo live it would resolve the
                    match while this poller is still writing.
                    demo-doomsday-deadhand is where the ending is the subject.

GREEN means the ladder climbed one step per exchange, the firer never climbed
itself, and no band was ever fireable inside its side's cooldown.
