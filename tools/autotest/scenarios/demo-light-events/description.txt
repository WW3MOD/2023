Demo: the generic local light-event system on a deliberately dark map (TerrainLighting Intensity 0.28). Seven different envelopes, one vocabulary, two emitters - six from the TimedLightSource actor trait, one from the LightEventWarhead. Schedule in ticks (60ms each, 16.67/s): t=1 looping flicker at 32,5, never stops; t=60 slow eased pulse at 8,16; t=160 sharp warhead flash at 20,16 (a bradley is killed, its Explodes fires the light weapon) - peaks the next tick and is gone by t=185; t=260 DOUBLE FLASH at 32,16, two peaks at t=262 and t=280 with a dark trough between, then 180 ticks cooling white to dull red while the radius grows 2c0 to 22c0; t=480 long slow fade at 44,16 with a SHRINKING radius; t=560 hard-edged cyan at 56,16; t=700 the 56-cell ceiling probe at 32,26 - radius 60c0, past MapGrid.MaximumTileSearchRange, which used to be a hard crash. Last light ends t=850 (51 s). Best single frame is around t=280. The demo halves the zoom at WorldLoaded, so all five row sites are on screen at once.
