An Abrams serviced at the Logistics Centre's crane cell must leave the building by itself once it is full, legibly (the move drawn in the automatic-order colour, since the player never gave it), so that a second Abrams can then dock on the same cell and be serviced too. Before 2026-09-05 the depot was 3x3 and its dock was a transit-only cell, so the idle handler shoved the tank off by accident; the 2x2 crane cell is stayable by construction and Resupply now queues an explicit vacate instead. PASS requires all four: full service on the crane cell, the tank ending up off the footprint entirely, exactly one automatic-coloured target line for that move, and the second tank serviced on the freed cell.
