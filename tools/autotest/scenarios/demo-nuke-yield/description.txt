Demo: the two nuclear warheads after the 2026-09-06 yield rebuild, fired 60 cells apart on a 128x128 map. Tactical Atomic (~20 kt) detonates at tick 20 at cell 26,104; strategic AtomicHighYield (~6 Mt) at tick 200 at cell 71,64. Watch tick 22 for the tactical fireball's second maximum (12-cell white disc), tick 203 for the strategic weapon's double-flash DIP - the one thing only a megaton weapon is slow enough to show - and tick 241 for its second maximum, the same peak brightness across 124 cells. Both weapons' blast waves are born at their own fireball surface travelling supersonically rather than at a point. From tick 411 both permanent scorch discs are on the ground at once, 24 cells wide against 94, which is the size comparison. Zoom out - the camera cannot be zoomed from Lua. Trees are indestructible in this mod and will not change; read the structures and infantry.
