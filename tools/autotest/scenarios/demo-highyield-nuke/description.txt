Demo: the high-yield strategic nuclear strike detonates at the exact centre of a 128x128 map. 81 identical target sites (structure + vehicle + 2 infantry + 2 trees) are arranged in nine rings from 6 to 82 cells out, so the damage gradient reads in every direction. Trees are indestructible in this mod and will not change; read the structures and infantry.
