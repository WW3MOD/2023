Demo: the generic heat-haze system - a screen-space refraction shimmer over hot air, driven by the same keyframe-envelope vocabulary as the local light events. Three events at three scales, all from the same code: a looping 1.25-cell burning wreck at 94,103 from tick 1, a 20 kt nuclear haze at 26,104 from tick 20 (2.78 cells, peak at tick 30, over by tick 92), and a 6 Mt one at 71,64 from tick 200 (27 cells, peak at tick 324, over by tick 1440). Nothing on this map is damaged and nothing dies: refraction is only visible against contrast, so the 487-actor target field has to survive to be distorted. Look for straight building edges that have stopped being straight - there is no sprite, no colour change and no brightness change. Best frames: tick 30 (tactical peak, 5.6 cells across), tick 324 (strategic peak, 54 cells across, at the SAME 9px peak strength - yield buys area and duration, not a stronger wobble) and tick 600 (the long decay). Two frames ten ticks apart beat one, because the shimmer is a motion. Zoom out for the strategic event, in for the tactical one; the camera cannot be zoomed from Lua.
