Two humvees are each given the same two shift-queued move waypoints heading EAST. One is then sent Shift+E (queued Evacuate), the other a bare E (immediate Evacuate). Evacuation always exits WEST, so direction alone separates the verdicts. The Shift+E humvee must drive east past x=20 to its last waypoint BEFORE exiting; the bare-E humvee must exit without ever going east of x=20. The bare-E arm is the control: it proves the Evacuate hotkey reaches the command bar at all, so a silent "nothing happened" cannot be misread as a queuing failure. A third humvee carries a deliberately weaker arm: Shift+R must be CONSUMED by the Resupply button (with bare R as its control), which was dead by the same missing-noShiftButtons-entry mechanism. That arm asserts reachability only and says nothing about how a queued Resupply behaves.
