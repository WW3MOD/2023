A damaged helicopter sent to a pad heals to full, leaves the deck, and is billed for it.

Two HELI at 60% HP, both owned by the human player. "Patient" is ordered to a player-owned
afld; "Control" is given no order at all.

PASS requires, at tick 1025:
  * Patient at exactly MaxHealth (800/800),
  * Patient airborne again — CenterPosition.Z above its own grounded Z — the wedge check,
  * Control unchanged at 480 HP and at its start altitude,
  * the player billed exactly 504 credits.

The bill is asserted exactly rather than within a band because rules.yaml sets
PassiveIncome: 0. The 2026-09-05 run (260905_171321) proved why that matters: it reported
"billed -1496", the purse going UP, which is 20 x 100 passive income minus the 504 the
repair really did cost. The repair was correct; the measurement was not.

The defect this pins: Repairable.PercentageStep / RepairsUnits.PercentageStep were unset on
every airframe and on both pads, so RepairTick's step resolved 0. An airframe at a pad
healed nothing and — because ResupplyType.Repair clears only at DamageState.Undamaged and
Resupply's only exit is activeResupplyTypes == 0 — could never leave. Fixed by
PercentageStep: 3 on ^Airborne's Repairable (mods/ww3mod/rules/ingame/aircraft.yaml).

The host is an afld rather than an hpad because hpad.shp and hpadmake.shp do not exist in
this repo (mods/ww3mod/lint-baseline.txt:353-354); rules.yaml widens HELI's RepairActors to
reach one. It never sets PercentageStep, so this still proves that ^Helicopter's Repairable
node MERGES with ^Airborne's rather than replacing it.

No fixed-wing leg: see map.yaml, and WORKSPACE/bugs/discovered.md [2026-09-05] for the A10
that never landed.
