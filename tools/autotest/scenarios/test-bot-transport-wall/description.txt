TEST: a loaded bot carrier is not stranded by the DEFCON 3 border.

WHAT IS UNDER TEST
------------------
MountedTransportBotModule.BorderStagingEnabled — the transport half of the same lever
PoiOffensiveBotModule has carried under that name since 45c43ffe.

THE DEFECT THIS REPRODUCES
--------------------------
A delivery is issued as a plain "Move". Mobile.ResolveOrder REFUSES a Move whose destination is
across the DEFCON 3 border and returns before queueing the Move activity at all
(engine/OpenRA.Mods.Common/Traits/Mobile.cs:1111-1114). So the carrier does not move one cell.

Every downstream condition then holds forever:
  - it stays IsIdle, because no activity was ever queued;
  - it stays outside DropOffArrivalRadius (3) of a drop cell it cannot reach;
  - CarrierState.Delivering's idle-recovery branch therefore re-issues the identical refused Move
    on every ScanInterval (50 ticks) for the rest of the phase
    (MountedTransportBotModule.cs, `delivery-move-reissued`).

The carrier and every soldier in its hold are dead weight for the whole of DEFCON 3 — the phase
those soldiers exist in order to take up position during.

This is NOT the same failure as test-bot-defcon-wall's. That one is about AttackMove, which the
engine ACCEPTS and then silently does nothing with. This one is about Move, which the engine
refuses outright. The two modules reach the same stall by different routes and need separate fixes
and separate tests; this scenario is the second of the pair.

PROVENANCE
----------
Measured on run 260913_233400_p91963_test-bot-defcon-wall: two carriers, 20
`[exp-transport] delivery-move-reissued` lines in a 500-tick window. The commit that closed that
run's own assertion (5dfad59a) names this explicitly as "a real defect, but in
MountedTransportBotModule, not here".

LAYOUT
------
  USA-bot SR      3,28    the bot's beachhead; also the transport task's Return cell, which is the
                          anchor the staging walk starts from
  Russia SR      94,28    unowned by any client; exists so the pre-contact drop lerp has a target
  carriers        6,26 and 6,30        two bradleys, empty and idle
  infantry        4..8,25..31          six e3.america inside the 14-cell reserve bubble
  border          x 44, HalfWidth 1024 ⇒ impassable band at columns 43..45

Nothing here forces the drop cell past the line. Before contact the module lerps from our SR toward
the top PoiMap offensive target at PreContactStagingPct (50 on both shipped twins), which with SRs
at x 3 and x 94 lands at x ~48 — eight cells beyond the band. The shipped tuning picks it.

GREEN CRITERION
---------------
  1. No carrier is ever observed at x >= 43 (the border holds — and a LOADED carrier over the line
     would be the worst version of this bug, not the fixed version of it).
  2. At least one carrier actually loads a passenger. Checked first and reported as SCENARIO HEALTH
     rather than as the defect: with no load attempted, a pass would be vacuous.
  3. Every carrier that loads advances >= 10 cells east of where it was standing when its hold
     became non-empty, within 900 ticks of loading. THIS IS THE DISCRIMINATOR.
  4. Every carrier that loads is empty again within 2000 ticks of loading. Softer than 3 and the
     one to suspect first if a GREEN arm fails here alone -- it covers a ~36-cell drive plus an
     Unload and no measured traverse figure for a bradley exists yet.
  5. MountedTransportBotModule queues <= 120 orders in the last 800 ticks (belt, provisional).

RED ARM
-------
rules.yaml: set `BorderStagingEnabled: false` under MountedTransportBotModule@experimental.

Expected failure, from criterion 3:
  "carrier 1 loaded at tick N (x=6) and advanced only 0 cells in 900 ticks (need 10): its delivery
   Move is being refused at the DEFCON 3 border and re-issued every scan"

Criterion 1 passes in BOTH arms — the stranded carrier cannot move at all, let alone cross — so it
is an invariant and not the discriminator. Criterion 5 is expected to fail in the RED arm too (two
carriers re-issuing once per 50-tick scan over an 800-tick window is ~32 re-issues on top of
ordinary module traffic), but the advance check fires first and is the one to read.
