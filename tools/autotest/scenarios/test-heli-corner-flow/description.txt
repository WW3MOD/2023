Does a helicopter keep its speed through an INTERMEDIATE waypoint instead of stopping on it? Two lanes fly the same 90-degree L in one run: LaneOn (HELI, shipped default) should release the corner ~4.1 cells early and arc through it; LaneOff (MI28, WaypointReleaseAggression 0) is the pre-change control and should stop dead on the waypoint. THE VERDICT IS THE FINDING -- it goes red on the reported bug, unlike test-husk-corner-slide next door. Pass needs BOTH: treatment min speed through the corner >= 122 WDist/tick (model says 180, control says 5), and a rejoin error -- SIGNED distance from the outbound leg's line once 8 cells up it -- within 1024 WDist either side (model says 516 east). Criterion 1 discriminates between the arms; criterion 2 does not and is a two-sided guard against a mistuned release, where the SIGN gives the remediation: east means released too late, RAISE the aggression; west means too early, LOWER it. Both thresholds are derived in tools/heli-corner-model/model.py, which replicates the engine's integer path; rerun it after any change to AircraftCornerMath. Numbers land in lua.log as [corner] RESULT lines. A failure raised by this file says STAGING-FAILURE; a bare "Fatal Lua Error" means it never ran.
