Does a helicopter keep its speed through an INTERMEDIATE waypoint instead of stopping on it? Two lanes fly the same 90-degree L in one run: LaneOn (HELI, shipped default) should release the corner ~4 cells early and arc through it; LaneOff (MI28, WaypointReleaseAggression 0) is the pre-change control and should stop dead on the waypoint. THE VERDICT IS THE FINDING -- it goes red on the reported bug, unlike test-husk-corner-slide next door. Pass needs BOTH: treatment min speed through the corner >= 122 WDist/tick (50% of cruise), and no more than half a cell past the outbound leg's line. The first criterion discriminates between the arms; the second does not and is only a guard against a mistuned release arcing wide. Numbers land in lua.log as [corner] RESULT lines. A failure raised by this file says STAGING-FAILURE; a bare "Fatal Lua Error" means it never ran.
