The Logistics Centre is 2x2 and services vehicles at the crane on its bottom-left cell. An LCCV deploys one and the building that appears must be two cells square, measured by its centre sitting half a cell — not a whole cell — from its top-left cell's centre. A dry, damaged abrams sent to a second Centre must end up standing ON that crane cell and be both repaired and rearmed there; without a declared dock it cannot, because an even-dimensioned building's centre is a cell corner no ground unit can occupy and the arrival tolerance for that errand is zero. Force-move (Ctrl) on ground then undeploys a third Centre back into an LCCV carrying its supply and health percentage, which drives to the clicked cell by itself, while a plain click must leave the building standing. PASS also requires the undeploy to take at least 44 ticks, which is what pins the slowed make animation. PASS also requires the undeploy to take at least 44 ticks, which pins the slowed make animation, and the first repair step to be at least 2% of the tank's max health rather than a trickle — that second one exists because repair at a Logistics Centre healed exactly zero until 2026-09-05 and merely asserting the tank gains HP would not have caught it.
