TEST: the bot breaks the DEFCON 2 cease-fire with its ground army.

The match opens AT DEFCON 2 on a 100x60 map. Eight bot tanks sit around the bot's
Supply Route at x 3; six enemy tanks sit at x 16-18, directly in the path of the
axis the bot forms against the enemy Supply Route at x 94.

DEFCON 2 permits any shot somebody ORDERED and refuses every shot a unit took by
itself. The provenance test is AutoTarget.IsAutoAcquiredSource, which is TRUE for
AttackSource.AttackMove -- and AttackMove is the whole of PoiOffensiveBotModule's
order vocabulary. The two squad-state sites that DO issue a named Attack are
unreachable for ground units, because every shipped SquadManagerBotModule sets
IgnoreGroundUnits and ground units are skipped before either recruit list is
appended. So before Defcon2DirectFireEnabled the bot's entire ground army was
structurally incapable of firing during the phase.

WHAT MAKES THE VERDICT ATTRIBUTABLE. The enemy is a slot with no Bot and no
client, and at DEFCON 2 its tanks' autotargeting is held by the same rule, so
they cannot fire either. The three non-ground paths that could otherwise break
the peace -- the fixed-wing and helicopter squad states, which issue a named
Attack, and the drone operator, which issues a ForceAttack the rule
short-circuits on -- are REMOVED in rules.yaml rather than merely left unused. A
casualty on this map can only be the bot's named Attack.

ASSERTIONS:

  PRE       One tick in, Test.DefconLevel() must read 2. If the Escalation mode
            or the opening phase failed to apply the level reads 0, the match is
            an ordinary Skirmish where nothing was holding fire, and the main
            assertion below would pass for the wrong reason.

  VERDICT   Within 90 harness-seconds an enemy tank must be destroyed AND the
            level must have moved to 1. The two halves fail with different text:
            a casualty with the level still at 2 means the kill did not qualify
            (DefconCasualtyObserver rejects friendly fire and neutral victims),
            which is a different defect from not firing at all.

RED SABOTAGE: set Defcon2DirectFireEnabled to false in rules.yaml. Expected
failure: "no enemy tank was destroyed in 90s and DEFCON is still 2. The bot's
ground army held fire through the whole cease-fire: every order it issues is an
AttackMove, which AutoTarget.IsAutoAcquiredSource classes as autonomous and the
phase refuses."
