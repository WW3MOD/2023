arena-tank-duel's terrain, verbatim: 66x34 of flat open ground with two spawns facing
each other down row 16 at x 6 and x 58. Fifty-two cells apart, and that number is the
whole scenario.

  THE BORDER    is DERIVED, not authored -- world.yaml sets DeriveFromSpawns and no map
                draws a line. It is the perpendicular bisector of the two homes, so it
                sits on column 32, and DefconWall's 1024 half-width reaches a whole cell
                either side of a cell centre. The band is columns 31, 32 and 33.

  THE PACKAGE   advances 35% of the way to the enemy: x 6 + (52 * 35 / 100) = x 24. The
                motorized annulus has an outer radius of 7, so its outer ring reaches
                x 31 -- the band's first column. The advance and the border are fifteen
                percent of the separation apart, which on a 52-cell map is 7.8 cells, and
                the package radius plus the half-width is 8. The clearance is negative.

  THE BUG       SpawnStartingUnits places at IWorldLoaded; DefconWall writes its
                CustomTerrain from its first Tick, which is after every IWorldLoaded. So
                the placement search's CanEnterCell test read open grass where the band
                was about to be, and a unit was put down inside it -- standing on ground
                that turned impassable one tick later and stayed that way until DEFCON 2.
                No trait order fixes this: the write is simply later than world load.
