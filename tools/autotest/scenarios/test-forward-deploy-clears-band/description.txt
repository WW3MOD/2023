TEST: a forward-deployed package never lands in the DEFCON 3 band.

arena-tank-duel's terrain, verbatim: 66x34 of flat open ground with two spawns facing
each other down row 16 at x 6 and x 58. Fifty-two cells apart, and that number is the
whole scenario.

  THE BORDER    is DERIVED, not authored -- world.yaml sets DeriveFromSpawns and no map
                draws a line. It is the perpendicular bisector of the two homes, so it
                sits on column 32, and DefconWall's 1024 half-width reaches a whole cell
                either side of a cell centre. The band is columns 31, 32 and 33.

  THE PACKAGE   advances 35% of the way to the enemy: x 6 + (52 * 35 / 100) = x 24. The
                motorized annulus has an outer radius of 7, so its outer ring reaches
                x 31 -- the band's first column. The advance and the border are fifteen
                percent of the separation apart, which on a 52-cell map is 7.8 cells, and
                the package radius plus the half-width is 8. The clearance is negative.

  THE BUG       SpawnStartingUnits places at IWorldLoaded; DefconWall writes its
                CustomTerrain from its first Tick, which is after every IWorldLoaded. So
                the placement search's CanEnterCell test read open grass where the band
                was about to be, and a unit was put down inside it -- standing on ground
                that turned impassable one tick later and stayed that way until DEFCON 2.
                No trait order fixes this: the write is simply later than world load.

Four legs, in order, each with its own named failure:

  GUARDS        The Forward Deployment lobby option really opened on `motorized`, the
                wall really stands, and columns 31-33 really read as Wall while 30 and 34
                do not. These come first because each is a way for the run to look green
                over an empty map or an absent border. The option is read through
                Test.LobbyOption and NOT Map.LobbyOption -- the latter resolves
                ScriptLobbyDropdown traits only and returns nil for an ILobbyOptions id,
                so a guard written against it faults on every run.

  GEOMETRY      The shape of the overlap, which is the deterministic part: the package's
                annulus is 68 cells, exactly ONE of them (31,16) is behind the border,
                and 67 remain -- far above ForwardDeploymentMinValidCells, so the retreat
                ladder does not engage and the overlap is real rather than something the
                search already stepped away from. Any change to the advance percentage,
                the package radius, the band half-width or the derivation moves one of
                those integers and is named here instead of quietly making the census
                below vacuous.

  CENSUS        Every unit the trait placed, read three ways: the terrain under its own
                cell, its column against the band, and whether it is in the annulus the
                geometry leg just measured. The second catches a unit placed clean
                THROUGH the band as well as one standing in it -- half of an annulus
                centred on x 24 is perfectly good ground on Russia's side of a closed
                border, and that is no better a place to start a match. The third catches
                a deployment centre that moved, which would mean the two legs are talking
                about different ground.

  PATHABILITY   Everything is ordered home to 12,16 and must have left the cell it was
                put down on. MOVED AT ALL rather than moved CLOSER: twenty units
                converging on one cell jam, and a unit shoved sideways by the one in
                front is briefly further from the rally while being perfectly able to
                path. This is not the census again -- a unit inside the band can still
                move, since the crossing guard permits any step that reduces depth. It is
                the pocket check: a unit clear of the band whose only way out ran through
                it.

Only USA deploys. SpawnStartingUnits spawns for Playable players, and a Playable slot
exists at runtime only when a client occupies it (CreateMapPlayers.cs:108-121 skips every
empty slot); an autotest launch has one client. The geometry is symmetric, Russia's side
of it is pinned without a game in OpenRA.Test/ForwardDeploymentBandOverlapTest, and
Russia here is an ordinary map player whose job is to own the far Supply Route -- which
is what fixes where the border lands.

PASS: twenty forward units, none in the band, none at or past column 31, all able to
leave their cell, over a wall standing where the arithmetic says and an overlap of the
measured shape.

THIS IS A GREEN-SIDE REGRESSION GUARD AND NOT A RELIABLE RED ARM, which is worth saying
because the obvious way to validate it is to revert the fix and rerun. The annulus and the
band meet in ONE cell of 68, so twenty units drawing from 68 cells miss it roughly three
runs in four: a green pre-fix run is the common case and proves nothing. What IS
deterministic is the geometry leg, and the arithmetic behind it is pinned without a game
in OpenRA.Test/ForwardDeploymentBandOverlapTest.
