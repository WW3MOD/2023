ASSERTS that a ground unit ordered to evacuate leaves through ITS OWNER'S OWN SIDE of the map -- the border its reinforcements arrive from -- rather than through whichever map border it happens to be standing nearest. Guards item 78's change to RotateToEdge.ChooseEdgeCell, whose ground branch read `FindClosestSpawnAreaForOwner(self) ?? self.Location` and now reads `?? FriendlyEvacuationOrigin(self)`. NOT AUTHORED FROM A RUN: this scenario was written under a launch freeze and has never been executed. Its geometry is derived arithmetically in map.yaml and checked statically with lua-gate (exit 0) and nav_guard.py report (2048 passable cells, one region, every ground locomotor). First execution is also its first validation -- if it errors on a binding arity or a hotkey name, that is an authoring fault, not a finding about the engine.

WHAT THE THREE OUTCOMES ARE, AND WHY THEY CANNOT BE CONFUSED. The tank stands at 58,16 -- six cells from the EAST wall and fifty-two from its owner's Supply Route at 6,16, with a RUSSIA Supply Route at 55,3 only 13.3 cells away. Bounds is 1,1,64,32, so Map.ChooseClosestMatchingEdgeCell sorting the perimeter by squared distance gives a strictly unique winner per origin, and the three winners are on three DIFFERENT WALLS: 1,16 (west) from the owner's Supply Route, 64,16 (east) from the unit itself, 55,1 (north) from the nearest Supply Route of any owner. The exit cell alone therefore names the origin the engine used, with no inference and no tie-break to worry about.

EXACT EXPECTED VERDICT ON A PASS. Test.Pass, opening "the ground evacuation exits through the OWNER'S OWN SIDE: the tank stood at 58,16, six cells from the east wall and 52 from its own Supply Route at 6,16, and its evacuation resolved to 1,16 on the WEST wall". The note goes on to disclaim what a pass does NOT mean -- it does not certify the drive is survivable -- and ends with the trailer, which on a clean run reads: Nodes at tick 30: [1,16]; tank was at 58,16; x at tick 250 was some value at or below 58; Evacuate consumed=true. Stdout carries two [evac-own-side] lines, one at tick 30 naming the node chain and one at tick 250 naming the position.

EXACT EXPECTED VERDICT IF THE CHANGE WERE REVERTED. Test.Fail opening with the words "THE BUG: the evacuation exit is 64,16, the EAST wall -- the border six cells behind the tank, which on this map is the enemy's side", and naming `?? self.Location` as the cause. That is the ONLY failure text a reverted build can produce here; any other failure string means something else broke.

THE OTHER GRADED FAILURES, EACH NAMING ITS OWN CAUSE. An exit at 55,1 is reported as the ally filter being missing, inverted, or applied after MinByOrDefault rather than before -- the enemy Supply Route is nearer to the tank than the owner's and only the relationship test excludes it, so this is the negative control for FriendlyEvacuationOrigin's `self.Owner.IsAlliedWith(a.Owner)`. An exit on the correct west wall but the wrong row is reported as a tie-break regression in Map.ChooseClosestMatchingEdgeCell (a floored CVec.Length sort key instead of LengthSquared) and explicitly NOT as a failure of the feature under test. An exit matching none of the three is reported with all three named and the activity chain attached. No target-line nodes at all is reported as the evacuation never having been accepted.

THREE OUTCOMES ARE DELIBERATELY SKIPS RATHER THAN FAILS, because none of them is evidence about the edge choice. A press the command bar did not consume (Evacuate is not a Lua property -- the only way to issue one is Test.SelectActors then Test.PressHotkey, exactly as test-evac-queued-line does) is a command-bar regression. A node chain of any length other than one means something besides the evacuation is in the activity queue, so the tail node cannot be attributed to RotateToEdge and grading it would be a guess. And a missing map actor -- Runner, OwnSR or EnemySR -- voids the geometry outright.

THE DIRECTION CHECK AT TICK 250 FAILS ONLY ON AN UNAMBIGUOUS CONTRADICTION. Under the fix the drive is 57 cells, about 50 s at the Abrams' Speed 70 and the mod's 60 ms Timestep, so the run ends nowhere near arrival and that is intentional: the node is the thing the change controls, and waiting for arrival would fold "did it pick the right exit" together with "did it survive the trip". How far west the tank has got by tick 250 is soft -- turn time at TurnSpeed 13 and pathing both move it -- so it is printed and never graded. Being EAST of where it started is not soft: it means the mover is going somewhere the node did not name, and that fails.

WHAT THIS SCENARIO DOES NOT COVER. It does not discriminate the owner's OWN Supply Route from an ALLIED one. FriendlyEvacuationOrigin accepts both and ranks them by distance to the unit, per the user ruling "possibly that they can evacuate at any allied SR as well, on their edge, if it is closer"; because IsAlliedWith is reflexive (Player.RelationshipWith returns Ally for this == other) the own-SR case runs through exactly the same MinByOrDefault, so this scenario does exercise the helper end to end. What is untested is the case where a FRIENDLY non-self Supply Route is nearer than the player's own. Covering it needs a third player and a PlayerReference alliance, an idiom no scenario in this tree uses today -- authoring one blind under the launch freeze risked a false "the ally clause is broken" verdict that was really a scenario fault, so it was deliberately left out rather than guessed at. It also says nothing about survivability, which is a balance question no single-unit scenario with nothing shooting can answer.
