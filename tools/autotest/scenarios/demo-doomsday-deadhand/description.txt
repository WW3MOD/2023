DEMO: Doomsday / the FINAL EXCHANGE -- the clock runs out, both sides get fifteen seconds to place
their own game-enders, Dead Hand places the rest, and the map is annihilated.

Watch for, in order: the countdown band ("FINAL EXCHANGE -- PLACE YOUR WARHEADS -- 0:15"); USA's own
1.2 Mt B83 going up inside the window with a beacon on the eastern city; the band clearing at zero
and "Dead Hand placed for: Russia."; four small warheads on the oil derricks; a distinct pause; two
large ones on the two cities; USA's B83 arriving LAST; then everything still standing is destroyed.
All Dead Hand impacts are near-vertical.

WHAT TO DO IF USA'S WARHEAD NEVER FLIES. The on-screen line at tick 265 says why: "not-ready:0"
means the power was never armed (the window did not grant it), "hidden" means the bin never drew
it, "unknown-power:B83Strike" means the arsenal is not loaded. Nothing is asserted here, so a
silent miss is the failure mode this printout exists to prevent.

NO FILL WARHEADS. The fill pass -- which used to top the salvo up until every cell of the map was
inside some lethal radius -- was REMOVED in the 2026-09-07 retune, together with the 20-cell lethal
radius that fed it (now 14c0). There is no third wave and there never will be one on this map; a
previous version of this file described both and was wrong from that commit onward. Ground between
the aim points is uncovered on purpose, and the sweep at the end is what makes "nothing survives"
true.

CAVEAT -- THE VERDICT IS NOT SHOWN HERE. A demo runs under TestMode, where ConquestVictoryConditions
deliberately never assigns an objective (ConquestVictoryConditions.cs, the TestMode guard in Tick),
so the win/loss resolution is a no-op and no "USA is victorious" line appears. The window, the
player-placed warhead, the freeze, the split, the ordering and the annihilation are all exercised;
the frozen-score verdict is the one stage this demo cannot show, and it needs a normal skirmish
launch to see.
