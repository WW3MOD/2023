CAPTURE INSTRUMENT (Test.Skip, grades nothing): five frames from one fixed camera of an ejected Abrams crew walking themselves off the west map edge.

The hull stays burning in place as the fixed reference the departure is measured against. Frames
02a and 02b are deliberate near-duplicates 60 ticks apart, bracketing a ~59-tick window; only one of
them has to land, and the tick schedule in the Lua carries the arithmetic.

WHAT THE READER IS LOOKING FOR. A TREND, not a tableau: three men who START near the burning hull
(frame 02) and are progressively further toward the boundary in frames 03 and 04, with the hull
motionless and smoking in the same place throughout as the reference. A single frame of men standing
near a wreck is equally consistent with the OLD behaviour, where crew ejected and then stood there.

THE SINGLE CLEAREST READING is the east man's reversal. The hull faces north, so the rear fan sends
the three men south, east and west — one of them starts out walking AWAY from the boundary. Between
frames 02 and 03 he turns round and crosses back past the hull. Crew that merely spread out and stop
cannot produce that. The facing was chosen to force it; map.yaml carries the derivation, along with
why the hull sits at 14,16 rather than a rounder 16,16 (which would have tied west against north for
nearest edge and could have sent everyone off the top of the frame).

FRAME 04 IS EXPECTED TO SHOW FEWER THAN THREE MEN. The fan gives the three different head starts, so
they reach the edge tens of seconds apart and the early ones have already crossed and been disposed;
anywhere from zero to three is a correct result and the printed readout says which. This is not
sloppiness in the schedule — there is no tick at which all three are reliably at the boundary, and
authoring one would be asserting a timetable rather than a behaviour. "Empty because they left" and
"empty because they died" look identical in that frame, which is why the frame 03 readout has to be
read with it. The actual failure to look for is men standing STILL in mid-field on the same cells
frame 03 showed.

Actor.Location IS ToCell. Every man is moving at every shutter here, so a printed cell can be one
cell further along the route than the sprite. Always at most one, always in the direction of travel.
A one-cell disagreement between readout and image is expected and is not the image being wrong.

WHY SKIP AND NOT PASS. The Lua evaluates no predicate about the mod. Its checks — that the hull
resolved and reports its authored facing, that the requested zoom was not clamped, that three crew
are alive at each shutter — are guards on the INSTRUMENT and can only downgrade the frames to "not
evidence". The behaviour itself is graded by test-crew-auto-evacuate, which also covers the part no
picture can show: that the evacuation is one-shot and that a real player order cancels it.
