Demo: two Iskander shots, to check the missile's nose lies along its own smoke trail. Trail = ground truth (puffs are dropped at the missile's CenterPosition). SHOT A fires north-west, ordered tick 30: ignition 110, climb frame 169, apex 218, descent frame 245, impact 253. SHOT B fires due west, ordered tick 320: ignition 400, climb 459, apex 508, descent 535, impact 543. SHOT B is a CONTROL and is unchanged - on a cardinal heading the art draws a facing at exactly its own angle, which is why an east/west check confirms nothing. SHOT A is the subject: the artwork is foreshortened 0.566 vertically, so a level missile on a diagonal used to be drawn 15.5 deg off its own track at EVERY point of the flight, apex included. Judge it at the apex (tick 218) - nose must lie along the trail, NOT parallel to the launcher's facing. Camera fixed at 66,67; zoom out one step before tick 110, Lua cannot zoom.
