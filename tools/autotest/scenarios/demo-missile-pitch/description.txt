Demo: two Iskander shots on different bearings, to check the missile's nose lies along its own smoke trail. Trail = ground truth (puffs are dropped at the missile's CenterPosition). SHOT A fires north-west, ordered tick 30: ignition 110, climb frame 169, apex 218, descent frame 245, impact 253. SHOT B fires due west, ordered tick 320: ignition 400, climb 459, apex 508, descent 535, impact 543. The old model over-tilted about 18 deg on A's climb and under-tilted about 5 deg on B's - checking one bearing only confirms half the change. At the apex the nose must sit exactly on the ground heading. Camera is fixed at 66,67 and never moves; the demo halves the zoom at WorldLoaded, well before ignition at 110. The descent lasts 35 ticks against the climb's 108, so it goes past about three times faster.
