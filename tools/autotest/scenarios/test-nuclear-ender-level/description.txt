Renamed from test-nuclear-ender-window at exchange v2 (2026-09-15). The bug is unchanged and so is
every assertion about WHAT the top rung grants; what changed is how a side gets there. v1 reached
END through a one-minute retaliation window; v2 reaches it because an enemy 100 kt raises your LEVEL
to 5 permanently.

USA (human slot, america) and Russia (map-authored combatant, russia) climb the ladder in the five
launches NuclearExchangeState.ReportLaunch's rules make the shortest possible chain:

  L1  USA  B61Low      300 t   band 1  ->  Russia level 2
  L2  RU   RuIskander 10000 t  band 2  ->  USA    level 3
  L3  USA  B61Max     50000 t  band 3  ->  Russia level 4
  L4  RU   RuKalibr  100000 t  band 4  ->  USA    level 5 = GameEnder
  L5  USA  W76       100000 t  band 4  ->  Russia level 5 = GameEnder
       (levels never fall, so USA keeps its own END through firing)

EVERY ONE OF THOSE FIVE ARROWS IS NOW WATCHED, NOT WAITED OUT (2026-09-19). This scenario used to
set `EscalationDelayTicks: -1` in rules.yaml, which restored the pre-2026-09-16 timing where a
victim's level rose on the tick the enemy clicked; it did so because the phase schedule was a
column of constants that fired and then read a level forty ticks later. The override is gone. Each
launch now snapshots Test.GetImpactEffectCount, waits for the detonation, and hangs its check off
the tick the victim's band was SEEN to leave `hidden` — so the file has no opinion about how long
a B61, an Iskander or a Kalibr takes to arrive, which is what let those constants be wrong.

Two assertions per launch come out of that, and they are the coverage this scenario adds:

  (a) the victim's new band stays HIDDEN on every tick between the launch and the explosion. RED
      the instant an escalation is applied at the click — which is what the deleted override
      restored, and what the whole 2026-09-16 ruling is about.
  (b) and is drawn within EscalationDelayTicks (50) plus a 40-tick grant allowance of that
      explosion. RED if the deferral never fires: a dropped pending record, an impact never
      reported by MissileStrikePower. (a) cannot see that — a level that never rises also never
      rises early.

Then it asserts, off the support power bin (SupportPowerInstance.Disabled — the predicate
SupportPowersWidget filters its icon list on):

  * USA's B83Strike reads `ready` at level 5, and can be ACTIVATED through the real order path.
    Both halves of "I should be able to choose the game ender and place the targets".
  * Russia's SarmatStrike reads `ready` at its own level 5.
  * The faction lock of 2026-09-14 (c8cadc8a) holds: USA never sees the Sarmat, Russia never sees
    the B83.
  * EXACTLY ONE END CAMEO PER SIDE (user ruling, 2026-09-14 — "national ender only"). The 6 Mt
    HighYieldNukeStrike names no owner, so it reaches neither side even with its lobby checkbox
    on and its condition satisfied.
  * THE RATCHET AT THE TOP: USA fires at L5 and still holds its END afterwards. Under v1 this
    pinned the window's one-shot SPEND rule; under v2 it pins that there is no spend at all.
  * Negative controls: no game-ender is readable at release, and level 2 never lights
    TacNukeStrike, whose host checkbox is off.

THE SIDE COOLDOWN IS NOT THE SUBJECT and is compressed to 60 ticks flat so it cannot bite: every
launch by one side is now a whole missile flight (257–605 ticks) after that side's previous one,
because each launch waits for the previous escalation to be observed. It is NOT set to zero, which
RulesetLoaded would refuse anyway — a scenario configured so the cooldown could not possibly apply
would also pass over its removal. test-nuclear-exchange's phase G2 is where the top rung's cooldown
IS asserted as a GATE.

ONE COOLDOWN READING DID SURVIVE HERE, and the retiming moved it to where it always belonged.
Phase G2 reads USA's own ender 40 ticks after USA fires at L5, and requires `charging:` — the firer
pays for its own shot at the top rung. That is settled when the button is pressed, not when the
warhead lands, so it is anchored on the LAUNCH. It used to live inside the L5 check at rise+40,
which only worked while a rise was forty ticks after a click; with the rise a flight away, a
60-tick cooldown would have long expired there and the reading would have asserted nothing.

The whole run is about 2400 ticks (~2.5 min at 1×), against ~560 before, and that is entirely
flight time: five warheads at 257–605 ticks each.

RUNS AT SHIPPED LOBBY CONFIGURATION AND THAT IS THE POINT. It does NOT set
PowersSandboxCheckboxEnabled, which every other nuclear scenario does — that option provides
`powers.event`, the prerequisite the three game-enders declare and no faction grants, so with it on
this defect is invisible. Do not add it here.

The match does not actually end: DoomsdayStrikeInfo.RunInTestMode stays false, so the final
exchange is inert and the verdict is not racing an apocalypse. demo-doomsday-deadhand owns that.
