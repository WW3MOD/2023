Demo: the RS-26 Oreshnik - six conventional re-entry vehicles arriving as plasma streaks, near-vertically, faster than anything else in the mod. Six passes, ordered at ticks 30 / 170 / 310 / 450 / 590 / 730, each preceded by a rebuild of a 4x4 grid of stationary T-90s on a three-cell lattice from 41,45 to 50,54 so every pass sees an intact target set. Camera fixed at 47,47 and never moves; the zoom ALTERNATES, 2x on the Oreshnik-only passes (1, 3, 5) and 1x on the comparison passes (2, 4, 6) - a near-vertical arrival at 6491 wdist/tick is on screen for only 2.7 ticks at 2x and 5.4 at 1x, so the tight zoom answers 'does it read as a streak' and the wide one answers 'is it steep' and 'which lands first'. Every pass fires the Oreshnik at 45,49. Test.ActivateSupportPower issues a SINGLE-target order, which is the bot/Lua path, so the six warheads are laid out by AimPointFallbackSpread (5c0) as one RV on the aim point and five on a five-cell ring - a fixed repeatable pattern rather than the six clicks a human would make. SUBJECT 1, THE STREAK, on every pass: six RVs drop into frame from the TOP of the viewport, not the side, and each should read as a bar of white light about four cells long (14 trailing samples at 288 wdist) with a blue-white bloom standing off the nose. PASS: it reads as a streak - the airframe is replaced with flat white at alpha E0 by WithHypersonicPlasma.BodyColor and does not read as a missile sprite at all. FAIL: it reads as an ordinary missile wearing a bright trail, or there is no plasma whatsoever (which is the specific failure mode to expect if MaxStep regressed - both WithHypersonicPlasma and SubTickMotionSmoothing treat a step over their own MaxStep as a teleport and draw nothing at all, silently, so "no effect" means the 12c0 overrides on OreshnikRV are gone or too small). THE NOSE BLOOM IS DELIBERATELY ABSENT ON THE LAST TICK BEFORE EACH IMPACT, about four frames at 60 fps: the leading sheath is clamped against the distance still to travel with a whole tick reserved, and at 6491 wdist/tick there is no room for it on the final step. That is correct, not a missing effect. The wake and the white body are NOT clamped and must still be present on those frames, and no part of the bloom may ever appear in FRONT of the crater. SUBJECT 2, THE SPEED, on passes 2, 4 and 6 only: a Kh-47M2 Kinzhal - the fastest thing in the mod before this branch - is ordered on the SAME TICK at 51,44, seven cells away. Both fly the same 197-cell standoff and rules.yaml equalises their MissileDelay to 15, so the gap between the two impacts is flight time and nothing else. PASS: the Oreshnik lands clearly first, by 32 ticks (1.9 s), and - the check that needs no stopwatch - ALL SIX RVs are down before the Kinzhal arrives at all (the salvo runs +62 to +82 from the order, the Kinzhal lands at +94). FAIL: the two interleave, they land together, or the Kinzhal lands first. SUBJECT 3, THE ANGLE AND THE FOOTPRINT: the Kinzhal crosses the frame almost flat at a 10.5-degree terminal slope while the Oreshnik falls through it at 56.25 degrees. PASS: the two descents are obviously different shapes, and in the target grid each RV kills the T-90 it lands on or beside while vehicles three cells away are left standing. FAIL: the Oreshnik comes in flat like the Kinzhal, or the grid is either wiped flat (spread too wide for "fairly low spread per projectile") or barely scratched (point damage too low). rules.yaml turns RequiresPurchase OFF for both powers because a script cannot buy one and the map has no cash - so a frame from this demo does NOT prove a player can obtain an Oreshnik, only what one looks like in flight and does on arrival; PowerPurchaseWiringTest and test-power-buy-loop are where reachability is asserted. Nothing about either missile is overridden here - speed, launch angle, plasma and warheads are all exactly as shipped. After pass 6 both powers keep recharging on a 90-tick interval so the viewer can fire either from the bin; the Oreshnik is the last icon, the Kinzhal the first.
