Demo: fast missiles no longer step between logic ticks. Four passes, each firing TWO Kinzhals at once on parallel tracks about 7 cells apart - one smoothed (shipped kinzhalmissile, carrying SubTickMotionSmoothing) and one RAW control (kinzhalmissileraw, the same actor with that trait removed and nothing else changed). Same Speed 2000, same TerminalSpeed 2400, same trail, same warhead: if you cannot tell them apart in motion, the feature is not working. The raw one jumps just under two cells (~47 px at 100% zoom) once every 60 ms; the smoothed one is drawn ahead of its last simulated position along its last tick's velocity, scaled by how far through the current tick the frame is. Both enter at cell 110,127 and fly north-west 100 cells (51 ticks, 3.1 s). THE TRACKS SWAP SIDES - smoothed upper-right on passes 1 and 3, lower-left on 2 and 4 - so a difference cannot be blamed on where it happened on screen. Order ticks 30 / 140 / 250 / 360; each pair enters +15, crosses the camera +40, impacts +66. Camera fixed at 75,92 and never moves; the demo zooms itself IN to 1.5x at WorldLoaded (further in would shorten the visible stretch to under a second). DO NOT PAUSE TO COMPARE: smoothing is deliberately inert while the world is paused, so both missiles sit on identical true positions in a paused frame - that is correct, not the feature failing. Also watch the gap between the missile's tail and the newest smoke puff: the trail is NOT smoothed and cannot be (LeavesTrailsCA drops a static puff once a tick at the real CenterPosition), so on the smoothed missile that gap now grows through each tick and resets when the next puff lands. Both powers keep recharging on a 60-tick interval afterwards, so the viewer can fire either from the bin.
