Demo: the control for demo-nuke-fog-seam. Same map, same weapon, same burst point at cell 64,64 -- but the map is NOT pre-explored and the CAMERA stands twenty-five cells west, so the explored disc ends at x=71, only seven cells east of ground zero. Fog and shroud occlude differently: fog is ARGB(160,0,0,0) and darkens, shroud is ARGB(255,0,0,0) and hides outright. The fireball should be at FULL brightness over the fogged ground west of that line and CUT OFF DEAD along it, flat black beyond with no dimmed ghost showing through. A cloud that fades rather than stopping means the unexplored layer went translucent; a cloud that carries on past x=71 means the effect escaped the unexplored layer too and is revealing the map. Glow visible east of x=71, or any actor becoming visible anywhere under it, is the failure this demo exists to catch.
