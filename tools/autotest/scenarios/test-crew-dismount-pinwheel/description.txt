CAPTURE INSTRUMENT (Test.Skip, grades nothing): six frames of crew bailing from four Abrams on four different facings — does the empty exit arc point where each hull points?

Photographs a crew bail-out from four Abrams carrying the four cardinal hull facings, so a human can
see whether the men come out of the BACK.

Six PNGs: 01 the undamaged four-hull reference (read it first, it fixes the facings from clean
hulls), 02 the wide result, 03-06 one close-up per hull. Verdict is Test.Skip.

WHAT THE READER IS LOOKING FOR. Around each hull the three crew occupy three of the four cardinal
directions and leave ONE empty, and the empty one must be the direction that hull points. Four
hulls, four different empty sides. The correlation is the evidence: the pre-3ce18d71 code rolled
`SharedRandom.Next(8)` with no reference to facing, and a uniform roll clears one hull's front arc
5 times in 8 — so a single-hull frame proves nothing, while twelve men agreeing with four different
noses is (5/8)^12 = 5.5e-5 under the old behaviour.

THE SHAPE IS A T, NOT AN ARC. DismountGeometry.FanOffsets is { 0, +256, -256, ... } and +-256 is
exactly +-90 degrees, so a three-man crew puts ONE man strictly astern and TWO exactly abeam. Three
men bunched behind the hull would be the wrong shape for this code. Grading a correct frame as a
failure on this point is the most likely reader error and is why it is stated three times.

WHY SKIP AND NOT PASS. The Lua evaluates no predicate about the mod. Its runtime checks — that each
hull reports its authored IFacing, that twelve crew are out and idle, that each is attributable to
exactly one hull — are guards on the INSTRUMENT. They can only downgrade a frame to "not evidence";
they can never conclude anything about the dismount. The geometry itself is already graded by
test-crew-rear-dismount, and nothing here should duplicate that.

The printed per-man offsets are a CROSS-CHECK, not the answer. If the readout and the picture
disagree, the picture is not of that state and neither claim survives. Reading the offsets as the
verification would launder MILESTONE-260901's ASSERTED grade into a SEEN one, which is the single
thing this scenario exists not to do.

REMOVE THIS FILE in the same commit as whatever gives the scenario a real assertion. There is no
obvious candidate: the question is perceptual, and the state half is covered next door.
